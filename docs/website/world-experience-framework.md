# Ogygia Website World Experience Framework — BASELINE

**Status date:** 2026-07-24  
**Purpose:** Reconcile the public website’s visual direction, interaction model, product explanation, frozen world canon, campaign system, progressive-decentralization mandate, deterministic incentive model, and public-information boundary.

This framework turns the current visual exploration into a repository-grade product direction. It does not approve a final visual design, create new canon, or represent concept art as a live product.

## Core model

The website should express three related ideas without collapsing them into one another:

- **Ogygia is the navigable world.** It gives the public website a memorable spatial and narrative information architecture.
- **The Odyssey is the person’s journey.** It describes movement through understanding, play, contribution, and eventually a private Living Chronicle.
- **The Promise is the social and constitutional contract.** It makes privacy, agency, evidence, public benefit, and institutional accountability inspectable.

The mythic layer makes the system memorable. The direct layer explains what the system actually does.

## Product-surface boundary

### Public website

The public website is the browser-first gateway for:

- project discovery
- Ogygia and the public story
- accurate product explanation
- trust, privacy, security, AI, and capability-status information
- public-domain and institutional documentation
- campaign provenance and public communications
- open-source contribution
- support and funding transparency
- a fictional-data prologue before account creation

### Playable application

The playable application is the later web, iOS, and Android surface for:

- the private Health Blueprint, expressed in-world as the Living Chronicle
- quests and deterministic progression
- Aster interaction
- personal capture, confirmation, reflection, and feedback
- permissions and access receipts
- persistent island restoration

### Contributor surface

The Open Forge is the contributor path for:

- repository orientation
- architecture and decision records
- public lore and content contracts
- synthetic fixtures
- validation tools
- public issue and contribution workflows

The website must not imply that these three surfaces are already implemented merely because their architecture is documented.

## Experience principles

### Wonder, then clarity

The intended emotional sequence is:

> Wonder → understanding → trust → agency → belonging

Wonder may earn attention. Understanding and trust must precede requests for an account, information, money, research participation, or authority.

### Narrative and direct modes are peers

The website supports two simultaneous modes:

- **Narrative mode** uses the island, characters, locations, light, tides, restoration, and guided discovery.
- **Direct mode** provides plain-language navigation, status, documentation, definitions, and conventional page structure.

Neither mode is a fallback. Every material claim, permission, limitation, status, and action must be accessible without narrative traversal, animation, sound, or prior knowledge of mythology.

### Every place contains a real function

A location is not a decorative lore chamber. Every visible destination must let a visitor:

- learn something material
- inspect a project commitment
- perform a bounded public action
- begin a fictional-data interaction
- contribute to the open project
- or understand what remains unavailable

Empty destinations should not be rendered as active places. Planned locations require a capability-status label.

### Personal value precedes collective benefit

The website must present the product in the constitutional order:

1. build and understand a person-controlled longitudinal record
2. receive useful personal feedback and support self-chosen action
3. optionally contribute to separately governed public-good or compensated opportunities

Research, compensated opportunities, governance, donation, and community participation must not be framed as prerequisites for core value.

## Initial website map

The first public map should not attempt to expose every canonical zone. A focused map should prioritize destinations that explain the project and create a coherent public journey:

1. **Lantern Shore** — arrive and understand the premise
2. **The House of Keys** — inspect the rights and privacy model
3. **Mnemosyne’s Library** — learn how memory, provenance, and the Chronicle work
4. **Athena’s Observatory** — understand evidence, Clues, uncertainty, and research boundaries
5. **The Open Forge** — inspect and contribute to the public project
6. **The House of Oaths** — understand governance, public-domain commitments, current status, and institutional accountability

The Hearth of Hestia may appear beyond the public boundary as the destination a person can choose after the fictional prologue. It must not appear to contain a real private account experience before that product exists.

## Homepage journey

### 1. Arrival

Use an illustrated night ocean, distant Ogygia, and one clear invitation. The hero should include:

- a mythic headline
- a one-sentence direct explanation of the real project
- one primary action
- one direct-information action
- one open-source or current-status action

The hero must not force animation, autoplay audio, or scroll-jacking.

### 2. The unresolved Promise

Explain Calypso’s realization that a gift without freedom is not a gift. Immediately connect the story to real product behavior:

- private by default
- purpose-specific permission
- meaningful refusal
- evidence before claims
- export, correction, revocation, and deletion paths

### 3. The real platform

Explain the three connected loops using both public and in-world language:

- build a **Health Blueprint**, experienced in-world as the **Living Chronicle**
- improve understanding through confirmed records, learning, reflection, and self-chosen action
- control whether information contributes to separately authorized collective benefit

### 4. Explore Ogygia

Present a map-based preview of the selected canonical zones. Each marker must expose:

- canonical name
- direct function
- current capability status
- one clear destination action
- keyboard and text-list equivalents

### 5. Experience the transaction rule

Use fictional data to demonstrate:

1. a person types or speaks one fictional observation
2. Aster proposes a structured draft
3. the visitor reviews and confirms or rejects it
4. deterministic domain logic records the fictional entry
5. an example access receipt is shown

The interaction teaches:

> AI proposes. The player confirms. The domain service validates and stores.

### 6. The Seven Laws

Show each law as both story rule and product behavior. The visitor should be able to inspect evidence of implementation or see an honest PLANNED status.

### 7. Current status

Display LIVE, EXPERIMENTAL, PLANNED, LONG-HORIZON, and DEFERRED labels consistently. The site must distinguish:

- repository and documentation that exist now
- visual concepts and synthetic examples
- planned product capabilities
- long-horizon research and institutional outcomes

### 8. The Commons and the institution

Explain:

- public-domain direction and legal-migration status
- the boundary between public project assets and private personal information
- progressive decentralization
- founder-independent success
- transparent campaign, correction, cost, and decision records

### 9. Invitation

Offer separate actions without manufacturing urgency:

- explore the fictional prologue
- read the Promise
- inspect current status
- contribute in the Open Forge
- join the founding expedition
- support the public-good project

## Paths, ranks, and public roles

The visual exploration proposed Voyager, Pathfinder, Scholar, Artisan, and Steward as role cards. These must not become permanent player classes or eligibility gates without a canon and product decision.

Use the following distinctions:

### Campaign interest paths

- future player
- builder or designer
- specialist reviewer
- public-good supporter
- writer, artist, educator, or storyteller

These route communication and next steps only.

### Adaptive gameplay contexts

The gameplay foundation defines temporary routing contexts such as Curious Explorer, Habit Builder, Symptom Detective, Condition Manager, Data Sovereignty Advocate, Citizen Scientist, Opportunity Seeker, and Caregiver or Legacy Steward.

These adapt the experience. They are not fixed identities.

### Narrative ranks

Castaway, Visitor, Seeker, Wayfinder, Chronicler, Steward, Investigator, Pathfinder, Promisekeeper, and Keeper of Ogygia are progression ranks tied to meaningful chapter transitions.

The public website may explain these categories, but must not mix interest path, routing context, rank, governance authority, or professional qualification.

## Visual direction

### Desired qualities

- luminous rather than grim
- Mediterranean rather than generic medieval fantasy
- ancient materials combined with precise modern interfaces
- warm, humane, and hopeful
- mysterious without obscuring function
- cinematic at key moments and restrained during tasks

### Visual vocabulary

- twilight sea, moonlight, reflected lanterns, and bioluminescent detail
- sea glass, weathered stone, linen, parchment, bronze instruments, threads, and constellations
- illustrated maps, architectural cutaways, provenance lines, and evidence diagrams
- elegant display serif paired with a highly readable interface sans-serif
- space, typography, alignment, and clear grouping before decorative panels or shadows

### Avoid

- dark-fantasy aggression
- combat-game framing
- casino, loot-box, or speculative-token cues
- dense video-game HUDs
- generic health-dashboard cards covered with fantasy ornament
- film-adaptation resemblance, assets, branding, or implied affiliation
- false product screenshots
- excessive parchment behind forms or dense operational content

The operational interfaces for trust, permissions, status, forms, and evidence should become calmer and more conventional than the arrival experience.

## Interaction model

### Illustrated map, not a 3D world

The frozen gameplay direction is an illustrated map-based narrative experience. The website should use:

- layered illustration
- hover, focus, and tap reveals
- short transitions between destinations
- persistent route and location context
- optional ambient motion
- text and list alternatives

It should not require a real-time 3D engine, free-roaming avatar, or game controls.

### Progressive disclosure without concealment

A visitor may discover detail through locations, artifacts, dialogue, and timelines. Direct navigation must remain available at all times.

### Return and interruption

Public interactive experiences should save only non-sensitive, local, or temporary state when disclosed. A visitor can leave at any point without penalty, account pressure, or a false completion countdown.

## Campaign integration

The founder campaign belongs inside the canonical website through `/voyage`, not in a disconnected microsite and not as a replacement for the product homepage.

The campaign layer should:

- tell the project’s documented genealogy
- preserve failed strategies with historical labels
- distinguish memory, documented fact, and interpretation
- connect every future capability claim to `/current-status`
- connect public-domain claims to `/commons`
- connect health-data promises to `/covenant`
- expose public strategy, campaign records, experiments, costs, reports, and corrections
- disclose independence from modern film adaptations

Cultural attention may invite someone to learn. It must never manufacture urgency around health information, research, donations, governance, or account creation.

## Trust and public-information architecture

The public website renders only purpose-built PUBLIC records.

It must not query or serialize private contact, signup, moderation, incident, security, legal, health, account, or correspondence records. Public records must use allowlisted fields, non-identifying IDs, version metadata, review state, owner, review date, and correction path.

No public form submission publishes directly to a campaign log, testimonial, issue, comment stream, directory, dashboard, or structured-data feed.

## Browser and mobile direction

The public website and fictional prologue are browser first because they require low-friction access, linking, search discovery, public documentation, and no installation.

The later Expo application is the universal playable client for web, iOS, and Android. Native mobile becomes most valuable when the product requires:

- frequent brief capture
- notifications
- camera and document input
- offline behavior
- HealthKit or Health Connect
- device-level permissions

The website is the island and public institution. The application is the private instrument carried through the person’s ongoing journey.

## Technical delivery direction

The frozen architecture selects Next.js for the public website. Implementation should preserve these boundaries:

- `apps/site` owns the public site and browser prologue entry
- public content is repository-owned or derived through explicit PUBLIC publication schemas
- shared design tokens belong in a dedicated package
- reusable web UI belongs in a shared web package only after real reuse exists
- canon and content objects remain validated independently of rendering
- private product and campaign-source systems never become build-time or runtime dependencies for public pages
- static generation or caching may be used only for PUBLIC records
- status and source links remain visible in rendered content

Framework versions, styling libraries, content storage, hosting, and analytics vendors remain implementation decisions. They must not weaken accessibility, public-information separation, replaceability, or the credential-free contributor environment.

## Accessibility and performance

The world is complete only when it works without its most decorative layer.

Required:

- keyboard-complete navigation
- screen-reader landmarks and map alternatives
- meaningful alt text
- transcripts and captions
- reduced-motion support
- high-contrast support
- no essential information encoded only by color, sound, position, or animation
- readable direct-mode summaries
- low-bandwidth and image-light alternatives
- no forced parallax or scroll-jacking
- stable layout and useful content before heavy imagery loads
- dates and status labels in text

## Concept-art status

Generated or commissioned website visuals must be labeled as concept art until implemented and validated. Before public release, each asset requires:

- rights and provenance review
- public-information classification
- metadata removal
- accessibility treatment
- confirmation that it does not imitate protected modern adaptation assets
- accurate capability labeling
- public-domain migration treatment when applicable

## Definition of success

The website succeeds when a first-time visitor can:

1. feel invited into Ogygia
2. explain the real product in plain language
3. identify what exists and what does not
4. understand the personal-data and public-domain boundary
5. experience the AI-proposes/player-confirms rule with fictional data
6. find trust and accessibility information without narrative traversal
7. take one low-risk, truthful next action

A beautiful site that obscures status, pressures consent, invents canon, or suggests an unavailable product is a failed implementation.
