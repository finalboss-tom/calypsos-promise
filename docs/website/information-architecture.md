# Public Website Information Architecture — BASELINE

**Status date:** 2026-07-24

## Purpose

The website introduces Ogygia, establishes trust, explains the real platform without concealment, preserves public project provenance, invites open-source and public-good participation, and eventually lets a visitor experience a fictional-data prologue before creating an account.

This information architecture is implemented by and should be read with:

- `world-experience-framework.md`
- `website-status.md`
- `campaign-entry-points.md`
- `open-campaign-transparency.md`
- `public-information-boundary.md`

The website must support two simultaneous modes:

- **Narrative mode** — immersive discovery through Ogygia
- **Direct mode** — immediate access to plain-language facts, documentation, privacy, safety, status, and contribution information

A visitor must never be forced through the story to understand what the project does, what information it uses, what exists today, or how to refuse an invitation.

## Foundational rules

1. Every mythic term has a direct, accessible explanation.
2. Every capability uses an approved status label.
3. Every active destination contains a real public function.
4. Personal value is explained before research, commerce, governance, or collective benefit.
5. The public website renders only PUBLIC information.
6. Concept art and planned capabilities never appear to be live product screens.
7. The website may invite attention; it may not manufacture urgency around health information, consent, research, money, authority, or account creation.

## Product-surface architecture

### Public website

`calypsospromise.org` is the public experience for:

- project discovery
- the Ogygia world and public story
- accurate product explanation
- trust, privacy, security, AI, and status information
- public-domain and institutional records
- campaign provenance and public communications
- open-source contribution
- public-good support and funding transparency
- fictional-data prologue entry

### Playable application

`play.calypsospromise.org` is the direction for:

- the browser-playable fictional prologue
- account entry when a private product exists
- the later universal web application

The Expo application remains the planned universal playable client for web, iOS, and Android. Domain boundaries matter more than whether an early browser route is embedded in the public site or hosted on the play subdomain.

### Contributor surface

`forge.calypsospromise.org` is the direction for contributor documentation and tooling. It may initially be implemented through public site routes that remain portable to a dedicated surface later.

### Operational transparency

`status.calypsospromise.org` is a later operational-status direction. Project capability status is required earlier through `/current-status` and must not wait for an infrastructure-status service.

Domain naming is a direction, not a dependency on any hosting vendor.

## Primary navigation

The main navigation should remain understandable without lore knowledge.

1. **Explore Ogygia** — illustrated island map and public story
2. **The Promise** — mission, product thesis, Seven Laws, and player rights
3. **How It Works** — Health Blueprint, Living Chronicle, quests, Aster, confirmation, permissions, and personal value
4. **Trust Center** — privacy, security, AI behavior, data lifecycle, access receipts, incidents, accessibility, and subprocessors when selected
5. **The Commons** — public-domain direction, progressive decentralization, governance, provenance, and current institutional status
6. **The Open Forge** — repository, architecture, roadmap, contributor paths, synthetic data, and public decisions
7. **Support** — founding expedition, donations, sponsorship, and transparent use of funds
8. **Play** — fictional prologue or later account entry

Utility navigation:

- **Current Status**
- **The Voyage**
- **Press**
- **Contact**
- **Accessibility**

The Research Commons remains visible as a planned or long-horizon direction and must not appear to be operating before its product, evidence, safety, consent, and governance gates are satisfied.

## Homepage sequence

### 1. Arrival

Visual direction:

- night ocean
- distant Ogygia
- reflected lantern light
- an illustrated, map-based world rather than a 3D game scene

Narrative line:

> Somewhere beyond the known world, a promise remains unfinished.

The hero must also include a direct explanation such as:

> Calypso’s Promise is an open-source, narrative-driven health platform designed to help people build, understand, and control a longitudinal account of their health and lived experience.

Actions:

- Approach the island
- See exactly what this is
- Inspect current status

A secondary Open Forge link should remain visible without requiring scroll.

### 2. The unresolved Promise

Explain Calypso’s realization that a gift without freedom is not a gift.

Connect the story immediately to real product behavior:

- private by default
- purpose-specific permission
- meaningful refusal without punishment
- evidence before claims
- inspection of permissions and access history
- correction, export, revocation, and deletion paths

### 3. The real platform

Explain the three connected loops in constitutional order.

#### Build the record

Public term:

- **Health Blueprint**

In-world term:

- **Living Chronicle**

Direct explanation:

- capture and import information
- preserve sources and timing
- review proposed drafts
- confirm, correct, and organize records

#### Improve health and understanding

- receive understandable personal feedback
- learn and reflect
- complete self-chosen actions
- explore bounded personal questions
- see uncertainty and source evidence

#### Control and optionally share in created value

- inspect permissions
- choose whether information contributes to separately governed public-good research
- consider separately consented compensated opportunities only when available

The first two loops must remain worthwhile without the third.

### 4. Explore Ogygia

The initial public map should prioritize a focused set of canonical zones:

- **Lantern Shore** — arrival, orientation, and fictional prologue
- **The House of Keys** — privacy controls, permissions, and access history
- **Mnemosyne’s Library** — memory, provenance, history, and learning
- **Athena’s Observatory** — questions, evidence, uncertainty, and research boundaries
- **The Open Forge** — repository, tools, schemas, decisions, and contribution
- **The House of Oaths** — governance, public-domain commitments, authority, and institutional accountability

Each map marker must show:

- canonical name
- direct function
- capability status
- one action

A text list with the same information is required for keyboard, screen-reader, low-bandwidth, and reduced-motion use.

Additional canonical zones may appear only when their public function, status, content, and accessibility treatment are defined.

### 5. The First Light interaction

Demonstrate one fictional observation becoming a player-confirmed draft.

Flow:

1. the visitor selects or enters fictional information
2. Aster proposes a structured draft
3. approximate or uncertain values are clearly marked
4. the visitor confirms, corrects, or rejects the draft
5. a fictional Chronicle entry is created
6. an example access receipt is shown in the House of Keys

This section teaches the frozen transaction rule:

> AI proposes. The player confirms. The domain service validates and stores.

Until the interaction is implemented, the section must be labeled concept or PLANNED rather than presented as a functional demo.

### 6. The Seven Laws

Show each law as:

- in-world law
- plain-language right or restriction
- corresponding product behavior
- current implementation status
- evidence or source link
- violation-reporting route

No law should be represented as implemented merely because it is constitutional.

### 7. Current status

Display these approved capability labels:

- LIVE
- EXPERIMENTAL
- PLANNED
- LONG-HORIZON
- DEFERRED
- RETIRED when historical capability records require it

The homepage summary must link to `/current-status`, where each capability includes:

- plain-language description
- evidence link
- last reviewed date
- responsible steward
- known limitations
- next gate

No planned marketplace, research, analytics, governance, clinical, compensation, or private Chronicle capability may be described as currently available.

### 8. The Commons and the institution

Explain:

- the public-domain constitutional direction
- legal-migration status
- what project materials are intended for the commons
- why personal information is never automatically part of the commons
- progressive decentralization
- founder-independent success
- public campaign strategy, cost, result, failure, and correction records
- canonical provenance without permission-based enclosure

Links:

- `/commons`
- `/voyage/disclosure`
- `/voyage/strategy`
- `/voyage/log`
- `/current-status`

### 9. Final invitation

Offer independent, non-coercive actions:

- Play the fictional prologue
- Read the Promise
- Inspect current status
- Contribute in the Open Forge
- Join the founding expedition
- Support the public-good project

The page must not imply that joining, donating, contributing, or sharing information is the required next step.

## Public prologue

The prologue runs entirely on fictional data and local or temporary state.

Flow:

1. Opening illustrated sequence
2. Arrive at Lantern Shore
3. Meet Aster
4. Read Calypso’s note
5. Inspect a blank fictional Chronicle
6. Speak, type, or select one fictional observation
7. Review Aster’s extraction draft
8. Confirm, correct, or reject it
9. Complete the First Lantern
10. Inspect a sample access receipt in the House of Keys
11. Choose whether to create a private Hearth when available or leave

No account is required until the visitor chooses to begin a real private experience and that capability is LIVE or EXPERIMENTAL under accepted release controls.

The prologue must provide:

- an exit at every material step
- no real-health-data prompt
- clear temporary-state disclosure
- manual and non-AI fallback behavior
- keyboard and screen-reader support
- reduced-motion alternative
- completion in under ten minutes

## Public forms

Initial public forms may include:

- founding expedition
- media contact
- correction request
- accessibility feedback
- security-reporting handoff
- support or donation handoff when approved

Every form states:

- purpose
- required and optional fields
- recipients
- whether submission is public
- retention or follow-up expectation
- deletion or correction route
- warning against submitting health, security, or other protected information when the form is not designed for it

No public form publishes directly to a public page, campaign record, testimonial, issue, directory, dashboard, or structured-data feed.

## Trust requirements

The website must disclose:

- what information is collected on public pages
- whether analytics or cookies are active
- which prologue state is local, temporary, or retained
- which AI provider receives what information once selected
- which features are unavailable
- whether a statement is lore, product behavior, scientific hypothesis, institutional direction, commercial hypothesis, or historical artifact
- how to report a vulnerability or accidental disclosure
- how corrections and publication-removal reviews work
- how donations and public-good funds are used
- the project’s relationship to third-party mythology sources and modern adaptations

The public renderer must never query or serialize private campaign-source, contact, signup, moderation, incident, security, health, account, correspondence, or legal records.

## Search and structured data

Use truthful project-specific titles, descriptions, breadcrumbs, organization, article, software-source-code, and creative-work metadata.

Do not use movie schema, studio entities, modern adaptation names, or titles designed to imply affiliation.

Search indexes and public feeds contain only PUBLIC fields. Private previews, form submissions, correction correspondence, incident evidence, and confidential source material are excluded.

## Analytics and privacy

Initial analytics should be minimal, disclosed, and activated only after retention and deletion rules are published.

Potential measures:

- public route views
- referral source
- current-status transitions
- source-document clicks
- founding-expedition completion
- outbound repository clicks
- anonymous comprehension responses

Avoid:

- session replay on trust, privacy, security, form, correction, or disclosure routes
- health-interest profiling
- cross-site advertising identifiers
- hidden pixels in health-related content
- inferred medical or demographic enrichment
- person-level ranking of journalists, creators, contributors, critics, supporters, or reviewers

## Accessibility baseline

Required:

- keyboard navigation
- screen-reader landmarks and meaningful alt text
- captioned and transcripted cinematic content
- reduced-motion mode
- high-contrast support
- plain-language summaries
- no essential information encoded only in animation, sound, position, or color
- low-bandwidth alternative to the interactive island
- text alternatives to maps, diagrams, and charts
- no forced parallax or scroll-jacking
- dates in readable text rather than image-only timelines
- accessible status and evidence labels
- no film knowledge required

## Performance and resilience baseline

The public website should:

- render useful direct content before heavy visual assets load
- avoid making the hero image or animation a navigation dependency
- use responsive images and explicit dimensions
- preserve stable layout
- degrade to a text-complete experience when scripts, motion, media, analytics, or optional services fail
- require no production health-data, account, AI-provider, or private campaign-source connection to build and run the public shell

## Acceptance criteria

The public website architecture is ready for implementation when:

- the visual target uses canonical Ogygia names and functions
- direct and narrative navigation expose the same material information
- the public/private product-surface boundary is explicit
- the first implementation slice is reconciled with the sprint roadmap
- current status is canonical and one click away
- concept, experimental, planned, long-horizon, deferred, and retired states are distinguishable
- public-domain status and legal migration are accurate
- public forms collect no unnecessary sensitive information and cannot publish directly
- public routes consume only repository-owned public content or allowlisted PUBLIC records
- core routes have keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, high-contrast, and low-bandwidth designs
- no modern adaptation assets or affiliation cues appear
- no unavailable capability is represented as live
- every major claim has a source, owner, reviewed date, status, or correction path appropriate to the claim
