# Public Website Information Architecture — BASELINE

## Purpose

The website introduces Ogygia, establishes trust, explains the real platform without concealment, invites open-source participation, and lets a visitor experience a synthetic-data prologue before creating an account.

The site must support two simultaneous modes:

- **Narrative mode** — immersive discovery through Ogygia
- **Direct mode** — immediate access to plain-language facts, documentation, privacy, safety, and contribution information

A visitor must never be forced through the story to understand what the project does.

## Primary domains

- `calypsospromise.org` — public experience
- `play.calypsospromise.org` — authenticated and browser-play experience
- `forge.calypsospromise.org` — contributor documentation and tooling
- `status.calypsospromise.org` — operational transparency, later

Domain naming is a direction, not a dependency on any hosting vendor.

## Primary navigation

1. **The Island** — interactive map and public prologue
2. **The Promise** — mission, product thesis, and Seven Laws
3. **How It Works** — Living Chronicle, quests, Aster, permissions, and personal value
4. **Trust Center** — privacy, security, AI behavior, data lifecycle, access receipts, incidents, and subprocessors
5. **The Open Forge** — repository, roadmap, contributor paths, synthetic data, and governance
6. **Research Commons** — planned public-good direction, clearly marked by current status
7. **Support the Promise** — donations, sponsorship, and transparent use of funds
8. **Play** — synthetic prologue or account entry

## Homepage sequence

### Hero

Night ocean, distant island, and the line:

> Somewhere beyond the known world, a promise remains unfinished.

Actions:

- Approach the island
- See exactly what this is
- Explore the open-source project

### The unresolved promise

A concise story explaining Calypso’s realization that a gift without freedom is not a gift.

### The real platform

Plain-language explanation:

- Build a longitudinal health record
- Capture information through voice, text, images, imports, and devices
- Receive increasingly useful personal feedback
- Control permissions and access
- Optionally contribute to public-good research later

### Explore Ogygia

Interactive preview of selected zones:

- Lantern Shore
- Hearth of Hestia
- House of Keys
- Recovery Springs
- Mnemosyne’s Library
- Open Forge

### The Seven Laws

A visually strong constitutional section linking story rules to product behavior.

### Aster

Demonstrate one synthetic input becoming a player-confirmed draft, followed by an access receipt.

### Open-source promise

Explain that code, schemas, fixtures, lore, and public-good infrastructure are open while production health data is not.

### Current status

Four status categories must appear throughout the site:

- Live
- Experimental
- Planned
- Long-horizon

No planned marketplace, research, analytics, or clinical capability may be described as currently available.

### Final invitation

- Play the prologue
- Read the constitution
- Contribute in the Forge
- Support the public-good project

## Public prologue

The prologue runs entirely on fictional data and local or temporary state.

Flow:

1. Opening cinematic
2. Meet Aster
3. Read Calypso’s note
4. Inspect a blank synthetic Chronicle
5. Speak or type one fictional observation
6. Review Aster’s extraction draft
7. Confirm it into the synthetic Chronicle
8. Complete the First Lantern
9. Inspect a sample access receipt in the House of Keys
10. Choose whether to create a private Hearth or leave

No account is required until the visitor chooses to begin a real Chronicle.

## Trust requirements

The website must disclose:

- What information is collected on public pages
- Whether analytics or cookies are active
- Which prologue data is temporary
- Which AI provider receives what information, once selected
- Which features are unavailable
- Whether a statement is lore, product behavior, scientific hypothesis, or commercial hypothesis
- How to report a vulnerability
- How donations and public-good funds are used

## Accessibility baseline

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen-reader landmarks and meaningful alt text
- Captioned and transcripted cinematic content
- Reduced-motion mode
- High-contrast support
- Plain-language summaries
- No essential information encoded only in animation, sound, or color
- Low-bandwidth alternative to the interactive island
