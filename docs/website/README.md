This directory is the source of truth for the public website experience, its campaign entry points, its public-information boundary, and the relationship between Ogygia, the playable application, institutional sources, and direct public documentation.

The public website is one of three product surfaces defined by the frozen architecture:

1. the public website for discovery, lore, trust, documentation, support, contribution, and a synthetic-data prologue
2. the playable application for the private Living Chronicle, quests, zones, Aster, and personal value
3. developer and agent interfaces for public documentation, synthetic fixtures, APIs, and guarded tools

## Current rules

> The website may make the Promise memorable. It may never make the real product, its status, or a person’s choices harder to understand.

> The website may explain future institutional interoperability. It may not imply that a provider, EHR, connector, sponsor, or enterprise capability is live, preferred, clinically endorsed, or authoritative merely because it is documented, funded, or planned.
