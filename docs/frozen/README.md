# Frozen Foundations

This directory warehouses components that are approved as constitutional or canonical foundations for Calypso’s Promise.

A frozen document may still receive corrections, clarifications, accessibility improvements, or implementation notes. Material changes to its promise, meaning, safety boundary, institutional mandate, or canon require an explicit decision record.

## Frozen in this baseline

1. Product thesis and player promise
2. The first player is people: the experience is broadly human, while routes, accessibility, goals, and pacing adapt to the individual
3. Open code and private health data
4. Player sovereignty and the Seven Laws of Ogygia
5. Ogygia’s world, central conflict, cast, geography, Seven Tides, and Fourteen Lanterns
6. Illustrated, map-based, narrative-first play
7. The Living Chronicle as the in-world representation of the longitudinal record
8. Aster as the AI interaction layer
9. AI proposes; the player confirms; deterministic domain services store
10. Structured health records remain authoritative
11. MCP is a guarded agent adapter over domain services
12. No reward for unnecessary intimate disclosure, no punitive streaks, no false scarcity, and no implied secondary-use consent
13. Personal value precedes research or commercial value
14. Progressive decentralization toward a self-sustaining, founder-independent institution is a permanent constraint; specific DAO, token, blockchain, identity, legal, and voting mechanisms remain gated

## Governing institutional documents

- `VISION.md` — the frozen long-horizon institutional mandate
- `ROADMAP.md` — public product, evidence, economic, and governance gates
- `GOVERNANCE.md` — present authority and progressive-transfer baseline
- `docs/decisions/0003-progressive-decentralization.md` — accepted decision establishing the constraint

## Status vocabulary

- **FROZEN** — approved foundation; changes require a decision record
- **BASELINE** — selected for Phase 0 implementation; may change through evidence
- **PROPOSED** — designed direction awaiting review or validation
- **DEFERRED** — intentionally excluded from the current release horizon
- **RETIRED** — historical language or mechanism that must not be used

## Retired historical language

The following historical terms conflict with the frozen sovereignty doctrine and are retired from public product design:

- Trojan horse
- Indoctrination
- Data vacuum, when used to imply extraction rather than user benefit
- Anonymous, when the actual protection is de-identification or controlled access
- AI doctor
- Guaranteed health-data value
- Uncontrolled clinical trial

Historical materials may retain these phrases for provenance, but new design and implementation work must use the approved vocabulary.
