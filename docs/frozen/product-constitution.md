# Product Constitution — FROZEN

## Mission

Calypso’s Promise helps people build, understand, and control a high-quality longitudinal account of their health and lived experience through brief, rewarding, narrative-driven participation.

The platform exists to return increasing personal value first, then enable separately authorized public-good research and compensated opportunities without removing control from the individual.

## Player promise

> Build your Living Chronicle. Improve your health. Keep the key.

A person can use the core experience without agreeing to research, commercial sharing, public visibility, or compensated opportunities.

## The three connected loops

1. **Build your health record** — capture, connect, normalize, validate, and preserve provenance.
2. **Improve your health** — receive understandable feedback, conduct safe personal experiments, learn, and complete self-chosen actions.
3. **Control and share in created value** — choose whether and how information contributes to research or compensated opportunities.

## Non-negotiable rights

- Private by default
- Clear, purpose-specific permission
- Meaningful refusal without punishment
- Inspection of active permissions and access receipts
- Correction of inaccurate records and memories
- Export in usable formats
- Deletion where legally and technically possible
- Clear distinction between fact, recollection, calculation, association, and inference
- Accessible participation regardless of physical ability, health status, wealth, or technical sophistication

## Product prohibitions

Calypso’s Promise must not:

- Hide its purpose behind narrative
- Condition essential privacy controls on payment or progression
- Reward broader consent merely because it is broader
- Reward unnecessary intimate disclosure
- Claim that a person’s data has a guaranteed fixed value
- Use punitive streaks, shame, fake scarcity, or dark patterns
- Allow an AI model to write directly into the authoritative Chronicle
- Present descriptive associations as diagnosis or proven causality
- Treat de-identified information as absolutely anonymous
- Expose production health data to open-source contributors or public demonstrations

## Meaningfully free

The frozen principle is that a person can create, maintain, inspect, export, and delete a useful Living Chronicle without paying and without agreeing to secondary use.

Hosted AI, storage, or premium content limits may later exist, but must not remove:

- Core manual capture
- Essential permissions and access history
- Export and deletion
- Safety information
- Accessibility features
- The ability to use the open-source software with synthetic or personally controlled infrastructure

## Evidence before expansion

Research, clinical recruitment, commercial access, and marketplace functionality remain gated until personal utility, consent comprehension, safety, security, and governance thresholds are met.
