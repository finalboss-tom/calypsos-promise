# World and Lore Canon — FROZEN

## Logline

Shipwrecked beyond time on the island of Ogygia, a modern castaway must rebuild a living map of their own health, restore an island being erased by the waters of Lethe, and discover why Calypso vanished after transforming her ancient offer of immortality into a promise that can only be fulfilled freely, collectively, and for all.

## Central theme

Calypso’s Promise is a story about memory and agency.

The body leaves signals throughout a lifetime, but those signals are scattered across memory, habits, devices, medical systems, environments, and institutions. Ogygia represents the possibility of remembering the whole person.

Calypso’s ancient failure was not merely that Odysseus refused immortality. It was that she offered a gift on terms that did not honor his freedom.

> A longer life without freedom is only a longer prison.

The new Promise must preserve agency as carefully as it preserves memory.

## Calypso’s final message

> I once promised forever to one hero, and I grieved when he refused it.
>
> For a long time, I believed he had rejected the gift.
>
> At last I understood: a promise that ignores the will of the one who receives it is no promise at all.
>
> I will make it again—not for one hero, but for all who freely choose it.
>
> The island has forgotten too much. Restore its memory. Learn its laws. Follow the light I have left behind.
>
> When Ogygia remembers enough, you will find me.

## The Living Chronicle

The in-world expression of the person’s longitudinal record is the **Living Chronicle**. It contains only what the player records, imports, or explicitly authorizes.

It produces Clues, not commandments. It grows more useful through continuity, provenance, comparable units, repeated measurements, context, confirmation, and recorded outcomes.

## The antagonist

**Lethe, the Grey Tide of Forgetting** appears wherever records become inaccessible, context is lost, closed systems trap information, weak evidence is overstated, or a person loses control of how their story is used.

Lethe manifests through four Shadows:

- **The Grey Tide** — fragmentation and lost context
- **The Golden Cage** — protection that removes choice
- **The False Oracle** — certainty without adequate evidence
- **The Masked Merchant** — exchange without transparent identity, purpose, risk, or terms

## The Seven Laws of Ogygia

1. **The Law of the Open Hand** — nothing is taken through coercion, confusion, deception, or manufactured urgency.
2. **The Right of the Key** — every person controls access to their Chronicle.
3. **The Sanctity of the Hearth** — private by default.
4. **The Law of the True Chronicle** — provenance, timing, method, and uncertainty must be preserved.
5. **No Oracle Above Evidence** — no claim may exceed its evidence.
6. **The Right of Return** — people may export, revoke, correct, and request deletion where possible.
7. **The Covenant of the Commons** — public-good knowledge must be governed transparently and cannot be silently converted into private extraction.

## Canon governance

AI may dynamically phrase dialogue, but it may not alter:

- The Seven Laws
- Character motivations and safety roles
- The meaning of Calypso’s Promise
- Zone functions
- The chronology of locked story revelations
- The distinction between Clues and medical conclusions
- Player rights or consent behavior

Material canon changes require an architecture or canon decision record.
