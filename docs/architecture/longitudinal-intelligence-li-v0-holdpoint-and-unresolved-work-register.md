## Purpose

This register preserves the gates that LI-V0 does not close and the work that must remain visible after the doctrine and validation architecture are accepted.

No holdpoint is satisfied by synthetic deliberation, documentation coherence, a passing test, a model result, a deployment, funding, or founding-steward acceptance alone when independent or operational evidence is required.

## Unresolved work

### LI-UW-001 — Canonical term and result-state registry

Convert doctrine terms and evidence-kernel enums into the future controlled vocabulary without creating runtime authority during LI-V0.

### LI-UW-002 — Exact LI-V1 measurement candidate

Select one candidate measurement package through rights, scientific-fit, temporal-unit, scorer, calibration, uncertainty, continuity, and provider-replacement review.

### LI-UW-003 — Synthetic lifetime and episode fixtures

Build representative synthetic Chronicle histories containing correction, conflict, missingness, source changes, context drift, response shift, and temporal uncertainty.

### LI-UW-004 — Descriptive-intelligence slice

Define the first LI-V2 capabilities, source classes, deterministic calculations, claim copy, uncertainty display, and non-AI fallback.

### LI-UW-005 — Protocol-family qualification harness

Create reproducible synthetic generators and operating-characteristic reports for one bounded protocol family.

### LI-UW-006 — Harm reachability

Define the minimum evidence and observation conditions under which harm can remain detectable rather than being suppressed by prior borrowing, refusal, or missingness.

### LI-UW-007 — Carryover and order qualification

Define intervention-specific carryover, washout, ordering, and partial-period rules.

### LI-UW-008 — Current applicability and expiry

Define source-to-target receipts, context change, authority expiry, successor protocol, and non-resurrection behavior.

### LI-UW-009 — Preference ambiguity

Define accessible, non-steering representations for robust separation, approximate ties, method sensitivity, framing sensitivity, revision, and unknown values.

### LI-UW-010 — Value of information

Define how act-now, no-use, defer, and eligible experimentation are compared without hidden utility weights or product defaults.

### LI-UW-011 — Human formative study

Prepare public or synthetic tasks for immediate and delayed comprehension, refusal, correction, deletion, export, uncertainty, mixed results, and authority boundaries.

### LI-UW-012 — Private-data gate map

Map identity, Chronicle persistence, encryption, key management, provider egress, logging, retention, deletion, incident, backup, and recovery requirements to later LI stages.

### LI-UW-013 — Specialist-review strategy

Name review domains, independence requirements, protected information paths, acceptance limits, and revalidation triggers.

### LI-UW-014 — Evidence-packet renderer and exporter

Design human-readable and machine-readable views without treating a UI, model summary, or provider response as canonical evidence.

### LI-UW-015 — Provider-independent replay

Define deterministic rebuild, adapter replacement, scorer migration, model replacement, and degraded operation.

### LI-UW-016 — Sprint 10 presentation primitives

Permit only synthetic and truthful UI primitives for source, uncertainty, claim level, refusal, correction, and status; prohibit health conclusions or inactive capability claims.

### LI-UW-017 — Phase 0 exit integration

Ensure the frontier-model-first architecture audit explicitly classifies LI stages, context modes, model and agent authority, private-data holdpoints, and build authorization.

### LI-UW-018 — Post-release monitoring and retirement

Define future drift, safety, accessibility, provider, measurement, and model triggers that suspend, expire, replace, or retire a qualified capability.

## LI-V0 disposition

The holdpoints and unresolved work are complete enough to preserve the boundary after LI-V0 acceptance.

They do not need to be resolved before Sprint 10 because Sprint 10 is limited to the universal public/synthetic game shell and carries HLD-LI-017. They do remain binding for institutional Phase 0 exit where applicable and for every later Longitudinal Intelligence stage.
