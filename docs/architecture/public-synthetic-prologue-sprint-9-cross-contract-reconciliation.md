## Cross-contract findings

### Product Constitution and player rights

**Result:** consistent.

The prologue requires no account, payment, provider, email, real health data, AI, or conversion. It preserves meaningful refusal, correction, restart, discard, exit, completion without an account, and temporary-state disclosure.

No choice creates punishment, shame, lost rights, reduced core explanation, health warning, countdown, manufactured urgency, or coerced conversion.

### Seven Laws and lore canon

**Result:** consistent.

Lantern Shore, Aster, the Chronicle explanation, the receipt explanation, and First Lantern remain bounded narrative presentation. The route cannot amend the Seven Laws, create new canon authority, unlock a canonical zone, advance the Fourteen Lanterns, or create durable narrative state.

### Architecture Foundation and application ownership

**Result:** consistent.

`apps/site` remains the sole owner of the bounded public prologue and `/prologue` remains the single route. A separate application is not justified for Sprint 9.

The implementation stays inside the existing modular-monolith website boundary and adds no database, CMS, queue, scheduler, remote content system, analytics platform, identity service, provider runtime, model runtime, payment system, or generic shared package.

### Public website and route status

**Result:** consistent.

`/prologue` is server owned, canonical to `/prologue`, `noindex`, absent from public navigation and the sitemap, unmerged, and absent from the production domain. It includes a no-JavaScript explanation and a direct return to the public site.

The protected preview is evidence only. It does not change the public capability registry from planned to live.

### Public and synthetic data boundary

**Result:** consistent.

Every fixture, identity, observation, transcript, draft, correction, projection, receipt explanation, and completion item is public or explicitly synthetic.

No free-form health text, microphone, file, camera, location, wearable, contact, provider, connector, account, or real-person input is accepted or inferred.

### Temporary-state lifecycle

**Result:** consistent.

Interaction state is React memory only. Refresh, navigation, tab close, restart, discard, or exit destroys it. The browser evidence observed no cookie, localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, Cache Storage, URL-state restoration, or hidden persistence.

There is no export obligation for temporary state because no user record is created. This does not authorize future private-state deletion behavior.

### Deterministic state and client authority

**Result:** consistent.

The scene and transition table is exhaustive and exported. Invalid or premature transitions fail closed and preserve the same state object. Confirmation, Chronicle inspection, receipt inspection, First Lantern completion, departure, and terminal completion have explicit prerequisites.

Sprint 9 creates no server-authoritative or durable gameplay state. The Sprint 10 handoff therefore requires a separate authority design in which no gameplay rule depends on client-side trust.

### Aster and manual parity

**Result:** consistent.

Aster and the direct guide share facts, fixtures, review controls, projections, completion rules, refusal, departure, and authority. Aster changes framing, not truth or capability.

The manual route is materially complete. No model, provider, prompt, memory, retrieval, tool call, network request, or AI confidence is required.

### Synthetic capture, review, correction, and confirmation

**Result:** consistent.

Text and voice use pre-authored synthetic choices. Voice is presented as a synthetic transcript and does not request microphone permission.

Confirmation remains disabled before explicit review. Prepared correction and fixture changes invalidate dependent confirmation and downstream evidence. Refusal remains reversible and non-punitive.

### Living Chronicle explanation

**Result:** consistent.

The Chronicle-shaped view maps selected accepted `ChronicleRecordEnvelope` vocabulary for explanation only. It is temporary, visibly synthetic, correctable, source linked, discardable, and unstored.

It creates no subject identity, authoritative record, longitudinal truth, health conclusion, provenance chain, correction history, export, deletion, or production Chronicle state.

### House of Keys receipt explanation

**Result:** consistent.

The receipt-shaped view explains selected `AccessReceipt` meanings while explicitly representing no request, evaluation, grant, consent, recipient authority, audit event, execution, or data release.

A real permission system remains separately gated and requires identity, purpose, scope, policy evaluation, execution boundaries, revocation, protected audit, explanation, and specialist review.

### First Lantern and gameplay incentives

**Result:** consistent.

First Lantern depends on inspectable synthetic evidence: Lantern Shore, guide choice, synthetic fixture, reviewed draft, confirmation or prepared correction, Chronicle inspection, receipt inspection, and deterministic completion.

It creates no reward, rank, health outcome, canonical unlock, Fourteen Lantern progression, account, permission, provider status, payment, or durable state. Reversal clears completion until the rule is run again.

### Refusal, restart, discard, exit, and account boundary
