## Reconciliation decision

Sprint 8 is internally coherent with the Product Constitution, Architecture Foundation, Gameplay Foundation, World and Lore Canon, Seven Laws, controlled vocabulary, Living Chronicle, House of Keys, Aster, Forge, security baseline, publication rules, funding doctrine, consumer-first and provider-independent boundary, operational-simplicity baseline, Institutional Immune System, contributor policies, and institutional roadmap.

The implementation satisfies the accepted Sprint 8 goal at the stated evidence level:

> Publish an honest, accessible gateway to Ogygia.

The review found no basis to:

- treat a website page, visual status badge, navigation label, source link, local build, preview report, CI result, sponsor record, provider reference, or public claim as upstream product, permission, clinical, financial, security, or governance authority;
- represent the site as a private Living Chronicle, House of Keys runtime, production Aster, remote Forge, provider gateway, connector runtime, clinical workflow, research system, payment system, account system, or playable Sprint 9 experience;
- reactivate email collection without the accepted provider, privacy, retention, deletion, abuse-control, incident, rollback, and deployed-verification evidence required by [issue #63](https://github.com/finalboss-tom/calypsos-promise/issues/63);
- claim a hosted preview, production deployment, accessibility certification, formal WCAG conformance, assistive-technology validation, affected-user validation, field performance, or deployed security verification from isolated local-preview evidence;
- make narrative traversal, animation, AI, an account, a provider, a sponsor, a donation, or MCP use necessary to understand the public project; or
- begin Sprint 9 gameplay before Sprint 8 is explicitly accepted and squash merged.

Founding-steward acceptance and squash merge remain separate human gates. Until they occur, Sprint 8 is ready for acceptance but not complete and merged.

## Cross-contract findings

### Product Constitution and player rights

**Result:** consistent.

The site states the frozen player promise, private-by-default boundary, meaningful refusal, correction and exit, meaningfully free access, personal value first, and public-software/private-data separation.

No public page conditions rights, comprehension, contribution, status access, or future personal value on account creation, email submission, provider enrollment, research, funding, payment, AI, MCP, or narrative traversal.

### Seven Laws and lore canon

**Result:** consistent.

`/laws` presents the exact frozen Seven Laws and states that the website cannot amend them. Narrative labels and Ogygia presentation remain explanatory and optional; they do not create canon, permission, progression, or institutional authority.

### Architecture Foundation and application ownership

**Result:** consistent.

`apps/site` is the single public website owner. Sprint 8 migrated the prior custom Node and HTML-fragment implementation in place rather than creating a second site, CMS, database, remote content service, shared website package, or service topology.

The site owns public routing, rendering, metadata, design tokens, public view models, build-time public-record adapters, headers, accessibility and performance budgets, and the separately gated signup compatibility surface. It does not own the upstream policies and domain truths it renders.

### Living Chronicle

**Result:** consistent.

The website describes the Living Chronicle but processes no production Chronicle data and has no Chronicle storage, correction, export, deletion, resurrection, or authority path.

Public pages, status records, synthetic fixtures, local-preview output, and future prologue data cannot become a person’s authoritative Chronicle.

### House of Keys

**Result:** consistent.

The site explains player control and receipt concepts but performs no identity, subject, recipient, purpose, grant, revocation, or permission evaluation.

A page visit, link, status, signup response, synthetic prologue choice, or website receipt demonstration cannot create permission.

### Aster and AI

**Result:** consistent.

`/aster` preserves the accepted governing transaction:

> AI proposes. The player confirms. The domain service validates and stores.

Aster roles are explanatory, production AI is not live, no model or provider is selected, and no private-data egress, memory, retrieval, queue, scheduler, workflow, or tool runtime is active.

### Forge MCP

**Result:** consistent and non-authoritative.

`/forge` renders the exact ten accepted local public/synthetic tool identities, their provenance, limits, receipts, errors, compatibility, holdpoints, and ordinary non-MCP contribution path.

The website does not turn Forge into a remote endpoint, private Chronicle service, provider gateway, general coding agent, repository writer, shell, production sandbox, or institutional authority.

### Gameplay and deterministic incentives

**Result:** consistent and deferred.

Sprint 8 explains the planned product but implements no playable quest, First Lantern completion, progression, rewards, Fellowship, Renown, restoration, game state, or account conversion.

Sprint 9 remains the first bounded public synthetic playable workstream. No Sprint 8 page, test, or local preview completes gameplay.

### Consumer-first and provider-independent architecture

**Result:** consistent and provider-respectful.

`/consumer-first` treats providers, standards, and institutions as important sources, destinations, and partners while preserving the person as the continuity layer and provider-independent Chronicle meaning at the core.

No provider, EHR, connector, clinical workflow, enterprise capability, preferred source, placement, ranking, or endorsement is represented as live.

### Public standards, mappings, and connectors

**Result:** consistent.

The site distinguishes standards support, planned connectors, public synthetic fixtures, active institutional relationships, and production exchange. It does not claim semantic completeness, clinical safety, conformance certification, provider preference, or active production exchange.

### Funding, sponsorship, and economics

**Result:** consistent with Decision 0008.

`/funding` is a build-time read-only derivative of the two fixed canonical public economics registers. The canonical registers currently contain zero accepted relationships and zero live opportunities.

Recognition remains separate from recommendation, provider ranking, connector placement, health guidance, permission, safety, findings, roadmap control, governance, and progression. No legal recipient, payment rail, checkout, donation, sponsorship intake, treasury, charitable status, tax status, or financial operation is active.

### Public support, challenge, and correction

**Result:** consistent with the Institutional Immune System.

`/support` routes public-safe issues, contributions, and system challenges to governed public channels while directing protected security, conduct, account, personal-health, correspondence, export, screenshot, and witness evidence away from public issues.

A challenge does not create authority, but a complete replacement solution is not required to identify a material defect.

### Signup and private information

**Result:** consistent with the selected interim disposition.

The public email newsletter or waitlist remains paused. `POST /api/join` returns `503 SIGNUP_MIGRATION_PAUSED`, does not parse or store input, sets no cookie, and forwards nothing.

`/privacy` and `/joined` explain the interim state and link to Phase 0 gate #63. Sprint 8 completion does not resolve final safe activation or retirement.

### Security headers, caching, and secrets

**Result:** consistent at repository and isolated local-preview level.

The site uses nonce CSP, frame denial, content-type sniffing prevention, strict-origin referrer behavior, permission-policy denial for camera, geolocation, microphone, and payment, cross-origin opener policy, disabled `X-Powered-By`, `no-store` API caching, and mutable repository-asset revalidation.

Validators scan public source and rendered resources for named secret patterns and prohibited runtime behavior. This does not prove hosted DNS, CDN, TLS, WAF, infrastructure, incident, or production-secret controls.

### Accessibility and resilient presentation

**Result:** consistent at implementation-review level.

Essential information is server rendered and available through direct navigation without animation or story traversal. The implementation includes skip links, landmarks, one `h1`, heading-order checks, visible focus, native keyboard semantics, accessible link names, alternative-text checks, reduced motion, reduced data, forced colors, increased contrast, responsive layout, image-failure backgrounds, and zero web-font dependence.

All accepted solid design-token pairs exceed 7:1. A founding-steward representative review is recorded. Independent accessibility review, formal WCAG conformance, direct assistive-technology testing, affected-user testing, physical-device coverage, zoom/reflow sampling, and field evidence remain open.

### Performance and low-bandwidth boundaries

**Result:** consistent at isolated local-preview level.

The permanent release contract enforces route-level HTML, JavaScript, CSS, image, font, total-transfer, and request-count regression ceilings. Exact 8.9 evidence remained below every ceiling, with zero web-font bytes.

The budgets are regression controls for the pinned framework and current pages, not proof of Core Web Vitals, device responsiveness, network performance, CDN behavior, or production capacity.

### Metadata, indexing, routes, and errors

**Result:** consistent.

Thirteen accepted HTML routes have canonical metadata contracts. Sitemap and robots behavior are validated, `/joined` remains `noindex`, API routes remain excluded, a public-safe 404 is tested, and `/api/join` preserves 503/405 behavior.

No route or redirect activates an account, provider, connector, payment, gameplay, or private-data path.

### Operational simplicity and operability

**Result:** consistent with Decision 0011.

The site remains one Next.js application with repository-owned public records and no runtime database, CMS, queue, scheduler, provider, model, analytics platform, remote content dependency, or transaction system.

A permanent CI job builds the production site, starts an isolated local server, validates routes and resources, publishes evidence, removes generated state, and proves no tracked mutation.

### Publication and confidentiality

**Result:** consistent.

Sprint 8 evidence is public or explicitly synthetic. No production health data, email address, signup record, credential, private provider negotiation, contract, proprietary mapping, protected security evidence, private financial record, or private support evidence is intentionally included.

### Institutional roadmap and Phase 0

**Result:** consistent and still open.

Sprint 8 completion does not complete institutional Phase 0. Gate #63 and the existing key-person, succession, founder-reserved-power, economic-dependency, historical-source, branch-protection, DCO, distributed-ownership, specialist-review, Decision 0009, architecture-audit, and phase-exit gates remain open.

### Sprint 9 handoff

**Result:** bounded and aligned.

Sprint 9 may implement only the public synthetic prologue after Sprint 8 is explicitly accepted and merged. It inherits no-account and no-real-health-data entry, disclosed temporary data, functional refusal and exit, deterministic authority, Aster proposal and player confirmation, a synthetic Chronicle, a synthetic House of Keys receipt demonstration, and an under-ten-minute target.

Sprint 9 may not silently become the universal game shell, authentication, private Chronicle, production AI, provider integration, connector runtime, research enrollment, donation flow, or health-data intake.
