## Evidence vocabulary

- **REQUIRED:** demanded by frozen commitments, accepted decisions, inherited controls, or Sprint 10 acceptance criteria.
- **DESIGNED:** expressed in an inspectable architecture, state, lifecycle, route, adapter, release, or failure rule.
- **IMPLEMENTED:** enforced by repository source or configuration.
- **REPOSITORY TESTED:** exercised by source, package, policy, content, state, and unit tests.
- **CI TESTED:** exercised through frozen installation, lint, typecheck, tests, site validation, exports, manifest validation, cleanup, and mutation checks.
- **RENDERED-SITE TESTED:** exercised against the existing isolated production-site preview and prologue browser journeys.
- **UNSIGNED-EXPORT VERIFIED:** exercised for browser, iOS, and Android without signing credentials.
- **ARTIFACT-PROVENANCE VERIFIED:** bound to exact source, lockfile, toolchain, platform, paths, byte sizes, and SHA-256 digests.
- **FOUNDING-STEWARD ACCEPTED:** explicitly accepted by the founding steward.
- **MERGED:** present on `main` at an attributable squash commit.
- **HOSTED/DEPLOYED/DISTRIBUTED:** running through an attributable preview, production, beta, store, or update channel.
- **OPERATIONALLY VERIFIED:** supported by representative monitoring, incident, rollback, and field evidence.
- **INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED:** reviewed by a named qualified reviewer outside proposing and implementing authority.

Sprint 10 establishes only the statuses named in each row. It does not establish acceptance, merge, hosted operation, distribution, independent certification, private product operation, or Sprint 11 entry.

## Control disposition rule

A control may be narrowed, superseded, or retired only through a versioned record preserving the prior objective, evidence, decision authority, downstream consequence, residual uncertainty, and revalidation trigger. Passing CI does not close an independent, production, legal, privacy, security, affected-user, operational, institutional, or next-sprint gate.
