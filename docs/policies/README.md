## Policy rule

The public repository may contain public or synthetic information only. A reviewed, minimized institutional derivative may be publishable even when its protected source material is not.

Performance, build, operability, or simplicity goals may not be used to weaken privacy, security, consent, accessibility, correction, export, deletion, clinical safeguards, specialist review, or truthful capability status.
