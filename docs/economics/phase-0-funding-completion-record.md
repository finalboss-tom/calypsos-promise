## Acceptance criteria mapping

### Funding doctrine and unresolved questions

**Status:** met.

Decision 0008 accepts the doctrine and keeps unresolved legal, accounting, tax, entity, compensation, investment, procurement, payment-rail, custody, and operational-control questions explicit.

### Funding cannot purchase authority or game progression

**Status:** met.

Donations, grants, sponsorships, credits, services, investment, affiliates, research funding, distribution, or other support cannot purchase:

- private data or participant access;
- research or product authority;
- health influence or clinical endorsement;
- source rank or Chronicle meaning;
- provider defaults or connector placement;
- roadmap control or guaranteed implementation;
- architecture authority;
- governance power;
- favorable findings, suppressed limitations, or publication control;
- safety, privacy, security, accessibility, or conflict exceptions;
- game progression, rewards, ranks, access, or superior personal-core rights; or
- permission placement, broader disclosure, or longer retention.

### Funding classes remain distinct

**Status:** met.

Donations, grants, underwriting, sponsorships, in-kind support, matching support, pro bono review, affiliate arrangements, investments, research funding, and compensation are classified separately. One label cannot be used to evade the rules of another class.

### Sponsor benefits are inspectable and bounded

**Status:** met.

Permitted and prohibited recognition, attribution, assets, events, acknowledgements, placement, content, links, claims, and third-party arrangements are documented.

Recognition cannot become product placement, source ranking, clinical endorsement, research endorsement, governance authority, provider default, permission pressure, or a claim that support proves product quality.

### Public records and private-source boundaries

**Status:** met at doctrine and contract level.

Organizational and restricted relationships require reviewed public records. Public records identify public-safe relationship class, purpose, value band or category, restrictions, conflicts, concentration, deliverables, status, review, correction, suspension, termination, and replacement information.

Raw donor identity, contact information, bank and payment records, contracts, tax records, negotiations, credentials, security findings, financial source documents, and protected correspondence remain outside the public repository.

### Ordinary governed work

**Status:** met.

Funded work remains subject to ordinary issues, pull requests, DCO, review, tests, specialist holdpoints, acceptance, correction, and publication rules. A funding deadline does not create authority to bypass them.

### Independent evaluation and recusal

**Status:** met at doctrine level.

Sponsors, funded implementers, related parties, and sponsor-funded evaluators cannot be their own sole independent reviewers. Conflicts, recusals, reviewer independence, and public-safe disclosure are required in proportion to consequence.

### Infrastructure replacement and exit

**Status:** met at doctrine level.

Infrastructure sponsorships, credits, free services, model access, hosted tools, connectors, and provider relationships require portability, replacement, concentration, migration, teardown, data handling, credentials, ownership, continuity, and exit planning.

Free or discounted service does not justify provider lock-in, hidden data access, architecture authority, a mandatory hosted development path, removal of manual fallback, or favorable evaluation.

### Concentration and critical dependency

**Status:** met at provisional Phase 0 level.

The baseline defines provisional concentration, critical-dependency, founder-subsidy, replacement-cost, and continuity triggers. Exact operational thresholds remain subject to real evidence and future governance.

### Suspension, correction, termination, and wind-down

**Status:** met.

A relationship can be paused, corrected, suspended, terminated, or wound down when it conflicts with constitutional rights, funding doctrine, security, publication truth, provider neutrality, evidence, continuity, or governing obligations.

The project must preserve public correction, source records where lawful, data and credential teardown, deliverable disposition, dependency replacement, and player rights.

## Incentive alignment

The funding baseline preserves this order:

1. people receive useful personal value and retain control;
2. public-good work may be supported under transparent bounded terms;
3. contributors may receive scoped compensation or recognition without gaining product or governance authority;
4. funders receive truthful acknowledgement and outcome evidence; and
5. no party receives authority merely because it supplies money, labor, infrastructure, distribution, data, status, or access.

Real compensation is never disguised as game currency. Game progression cannot be purchased or conditioned on funding, provider choice, sponsor interaction, research participation, or broader permission.

## Public and synthetic evidence

The merged workstream contains:

- six fictional opportunity records;
- sixteen fictional design-tabletop scenarios;

These examples are design evidence only. They do not represent real donors, sponsors, grants, contracts, providers, opportunities, payments, or financial operations.

## Independence and review limits

The founding steward proposed and accepted the baseline. Founding-steward review is not independent legal, accounting, tax, financial-control, procurement, nonprofit, investment, compensation, conflict, security, privacy, research, or governance review.

Before operating a material funding relationship, the project must obtain review appropriate to the relationship and jurisdiction, record conflicts, identify the authorized recipient and control owners, and create the required public and protected records.

## Deferred and unresolved register

The baseline deliberately leaves unresolved:

- legal entity and governance structure;
- nonprofit, charitable, tax-exempt, public-benefit, fiscal-sponsor, or for-profit status;
- banking, payment, accounting, custody, internal control, audit, budgeting, and financial reporting;
- compensation, benefits, contracting, payroll, employment, independent-contractor, grant, prize, and bounty operation;
- donation, sponsorship, grant, investment, affiliate, research-funding, procurement, and underwriting programs;
- revenue recognition, restricted funds, tax receipts, deductibility, cross-border support, sanctions, anti-money-laundering, and reporting;
- founder subsidy, related-party, intellectual-property, public-domain, and entity transactions;
- exact concentration and critical-dependency thresholds after real evidence exists;
- financial succession, emergency access, segregation of duties, and founder-absence controls; and
- independent specialist review.

## Future operating gate

Before a real relationship is accepted or represented as active, create a separately reviewed implementation record covering:

- authorized recipient and decision authority;
- relationship class and purpose;
- value, restrictions, duration, deliverables, and termination;
- conflicts, recusals, and independent review;
- private source-record custody;
- public ledger derivative;
- banking, payment, accounting, tax, legal, and control obligations;
- security, privacy, credentials, data, and provider access;
- concentration, dependency, replacement, migration, and exit;
- correction, suspension, termination, and wind-down; and
- evidence that personal value, provider neutrality, publication truth, and project rights remain protected.
