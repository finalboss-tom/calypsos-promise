# Public Campaign Decision, Outreach, and Experiment Log

**Status:** LIVE institutional-memory template  
**Purpose:** Preserve what the project tried, why it tried it, what happened, and what changed.

This log is the operating companion to `marketing-and-pr-strategy.md`.

The strategy explains the system. This log records its use.

## Publication rule

Material marketing and public-relations decisions should be logged when they are:

- proposed
- approved
- launched
- materially changed
- paused
- corrected
- completed
- retired

Results should be added even when they are weak, negative, ambiguous, embarrassing, or contrary to the original hypothesis.

## Privacy and safety boundary

This log may publish:

- public outlet and creator names
- public URLs
- campaign dates
- strategy and story angles
- aggregate costs
- aggregate outcomes
- public replies and corrections
- decisions and lessons

This log must not publish:

- private email addresses or phone numbers
- unpublished correspondence without permission
- off-the-record information
- confidential source identities
- personal signup records
- personal health information
- small-cell analytics that could identify people
- security or moderation details that materially enable abuse
- private notes that could direct harassment toward a journalist, creator, contributor, critic, or community member

## Status vocabulary

Use one status:

- `proposed`
- `approved`
- `scheduled`
- `active`
- `paused`
- `completed`
- `corrected`
- `retired`
- `cancelled`

Use one evidence state:

- `no-result-yet`
- `directional`
- `measured`
- `inconclusive`
- `invalidated`

## Initial record

### Campaign: Leave the Island in Public

- **Record ID:** `campaign.2026-07-24.leave-the-island`
- **Status:** approved
- **Evidence state:** no-result-yet
- **Owner:** Founding steward
- **Created:** 2026-07-24
- **Last updated:** 2026-07-24
- **Launch window:** Late July through mid-August 2026
- **Current public URL:** Not yet implemented
- **Related repository issue or pull request:** Issue #12 and branch `docs/public-domain-campaign-system`

#### Context

A major modern adaptation has returned the Odyssey to global public conversation. Calypso’s Promise was independently developed over several years from public-domain mythology, earlier HealthDAO and CureDAO work, public design experiments, and lessons about premature decentralization.

The cultural moment creates a temporary shared vocabulary for explaining Ogygia, Calypso, memory, agency, mortality, return, and the commons.

#### Objective

Reveal the project’s origin, failures, constitutional foundations, public-domain direction, and current state; invite people to inspect and follow the founding expedition.

#### Non-objectives

- do not announce a completed health product
- do not imply film affiliation
- do not request health information
- do not use controversy as the project identity
- do not solicit investment
- do not make donation claims before the receiving structure is ready

#### Audience

- future players
- builders and designers
- specialist reviewers
- public-good supporters
- technology, culture, mythology, governance, and health-data writers

#### Hypothesis

> We believe that people already discussing the Odyssey will understand Calypso’s Promise more quickly when the founder openly connects the ancient public-domain story to the project’s documented multi-year evolution, because the cultural moment supplies a shared language without creating the project’s underlying reason to exist.

#### Message

- Core statement: Calypso’s Promise has already had its own odyssey and is now leaving the island in public.
- Supporting proof: dated public design work, project genealogy, repository decisions, frozen foundations, and current public branch.
- Current-status disclosure: documentation, governance, schemas, synthetic examples, and a minimal site exist; the consumer Living Chronicle is planned rather than live.
- Relevant limitation: constitutional public-domain direction is accepted, while legal dedication and third-party review remain in progress.
- Call to action: inspect the project and join the founding expedition for public updates and bounded contribution opportunities.

#### Assets

- `founder-origin-essay.md`
- `launch-kit.md`
- `campaign-system.md`
- `marketing-and-pr-strategy.md`
- `attention-window-2026-07.md`
- `content-calendar-2026-07-27.md`
- `PUBLIC_DOMAIN.md`
- Decision 0004
- campaign website entry-point specification

#### Current-event relationship

- Event or attention cycle: theatrical release and public discussion of a modern Odyssey adaptation
- Preexisting project relationship: ancient Odyssey characters, settings, questions, and public-domain status have been foundational from the beginning
- Independence disclosure: independent project with no affiliation to any modern film adaptation
- Sunset date: stop leading with the film cycle after 2026-08-07 unless a new, independently relevant event justifies an updated dated brief
- Durable value: the origin, public-domain, health-data, governance, and failure-evolution materials remain useful without the film

#### Resources

- Cash cost: $0 committed at strategy publication
- Paid distribution: none approved
- Founder time estimate: to be recorded
- Contributor time estimate: AI-assisted repository drafting plus founder review; human accountability remains with the founding steward

#### Measurement plan

- Primary measure: informed founding-expedition participation
- Comprehension check: visitors can distinguish current status, public project materials, private personal data, and modern-film independence
- Guardrails: no affiliation confusion, health-data submissions, misleading capability claims, harassment, or rushed funding requests
- Decision date: after the first seven days and again after the twenty-one-day sequence

#### Decision

Proceed only after the canonical campaign doorway, current-status surface, privacy-respecting signup, original visuals, and publication review are ready.

The strategic documents may be public before the full campaign begins. Publishing the machinery is itself Stage 0.
