## Core asset specifications

### Founder portrait

Mood: candid, resolute, not corporate. The image should communicate that the founder is reopening long-term work rather than announcing a polished startup launch.

Avoid:

- imitation of a film poster
- armor, costumes, or actor resemblance
- artificial founder hero worship
- medical settings that imply clinical authority

### Project timeline

Required milestones:

- 2021 ConstitutionDAO inspiration
- HealthDAO
- 99designs public experiments
- CureDAO intersection
- realization and retreat
- emergence of Calypso’s Promise
- frozen foundations
- public-domain commons decision
- current campaign

Each milestone should include what changed, not merely a date.

### Old-versus-current comparison

Use a two-column structure:

| Earlier thesis | Current constitutional position |
| --- | --- |
| Sell access to health data | Personal value first; separately authorized compensated opportunities later |
| Ownership as an economic question | Control includes comprehension, privacy, receipts, refusal, correction, export, and exit |
| DAO as starting structure | Progressive decentralization through evidence gates |
| Brand and software protected through IP | Original project materials dedicated to the public-domain commons |

Do not mock or caricature the earlier work.

## Channel adaptation

### LinkedIn

Use the founder and institutional lanes. Longer posts may explain failure, public-domain strategy, and progressive decentralization.

### X

Use short threads, diagrams, and responses to relevant current events. Avoid prolonged culture-war engagement.

### Reddit and technical communities

Lead with the repository, current status, explicit open questions, and bounded review requests. Do not use pure promotional language.

### Instagram and visual platforms

Lead with Ogygia, the Seven Laws, timeline artifacts, and simple public/private contrasts. Every visual post needs a plain-language caption.

### GitHub

Use release notes, decision records, issue templates, captain’s logs, and public corrections. GitHub is evidence, not merely a destination link.

### Email

Send one weekly Captain’s Log:

- what changed
- what was learned
- what failed
- what remains uncertain
- one artifact to inspect
- one bounded action

## Daily response rhythm

### Morning

- Check relevant news and project mentions.
- Confirm no inaccurate affiliation or product-status claims are spreading.
- Select one planned message rather than chasing every trend.

### After publication

- Answer high-signal questions first.
- Route security and personal-health disclosures out of public channels.
- Record recurring confusion for website and FAQ updates.
- Do not reward harassment with prolonged attention.

### End of day

- Save material questions and corrections.
- Update the campaign issue log.
- Note qualified contributors, reviewers, and supporters.
- Measure comprehension and trust signals before reach.

## Campaign issue log categories

Track:

- product-status confusion
- film-affiliation confusion
- public-domain versus private-data confusion
- old-design interpretation
- DAO or token assumptions
- medical or longevity claims attributed to the project
- contribution friction
- accessibility problems
- press inquiries
- potential partnerships
- harassment and moderation events

Every repeated confusion is a documentation defect until proven otherwise.

## Exit criteria for the initial campaign

The initial campaign may transition into normal project communications when:

- the founding-expedition list has a stable onboarding sequence
- the top recurring questions are answered canonically
- the project no longer depends on the film as a traffic source
- the repository has bounded contributor tasks
- current-status language remains accurate across channels
- public-domain migration work has a named owner and tracked gates
- at least one original Ogygia experience or synthetic prologue artifact is public

## Post-campaign cadence

After August 16:

- Weekly: Captain’s Log
- Biweekly: one deep product, trust, or institution essay
- Monthly: current-status and roadmap update
- Per decision: public ADR explanation
- Per milestone: original visual or synthetic demonstration
- Per failure: documented correction and lesson

The project should become known for visible institutional memory, not constant promotional volume.
