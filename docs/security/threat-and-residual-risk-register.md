## Purpose

This register gives stable identities to the integrated security and privacy threats and residual risks within Sprint 5.

Each entry includes:

- threat or failure identity;
- residual-risk identity;
- actor, precondition, or entry point;
- assets, authorities, zones, crossings, sessions, or flows affected;
- protected properties and likely harm;
- control objectives;
- current evidence and uncertainty;
- disposition and gate;
- owner or future owner role; and
- revalidation trigger.

The register uses qualitative gates rather than a false-precision aggregate score.

## Disposition vocabulary

- **MITIGATION REQUIRED — PRODUCTION-BLOCKING:** production personal data, authority, or recipient access cannot proceed until the named controls and evidence exist.
- **MITIGATION REQUIRED — PILOT-BLOCKING:** no real-user, external-recipient, agent, connector, analytics, or research pilot until the bounded controls and evidence exist.
- **ACCEPTED FOR CURRENT BOUNDED FLOW:** accepted only for the explicitly named current public or purpose-limited flow, with current owner and restrictions; no scope expansion.
- **UNRESOLVED INSTITUTIONAL GATE:** requires institutional, succession, governance, or authority-transfer evidence.
- **SPECIALIST HOLDPOINT:** requires qualified security, privacy, accessibility, legal, clinical, research, or other review.
- **MONITOR AND REVALIDATE:** bounded for architecture work only and reviewed at the named trigger.

## Revision and closure rules

A threat or risk may be corrected or superseded only through a new revision that preserves history and identifies:

- prior and new claims;
- affected assets, boundaries, identities, controls, incidents, and public statements;
- evidence and reviewer;
- effective time;
- remaining uncertainty;
- new disposition; and
- next revalidation trigger.

A risk is not closed because a provider advertises a feature, an implementation exists in a branch, a synthetic test passes, or no incident has yet been observed.
