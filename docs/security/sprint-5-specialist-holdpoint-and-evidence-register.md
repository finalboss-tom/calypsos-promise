**Scope:** specialist, institutional, implementation, operational, and public-claim gates after Sprint 5 design completion

## Purpose

Sprint completion does not authorize a capability whose safety, legitimacy, or operational evidence remains unresolved.

This register gives every major unresolved gate:

- a stable identity;
- the capability or claim it blocks;
- the current evidence state;
- an accountable future role;
- the minimum closure evidence;
- a revalidation trigger; and
- an explicit disposition.

A holdpoint may be narrowed or superseded only through a versioned record preserving the prior scope, decision authority, evidence, remaining uncertainty, and downstream consequences.

## Status vocabulary

- **OPEN — PRODUCTION-BLOCKING:** the capability may not process real protected information or perform consequential production actions.
- **OPEN — PILOT-BLOCKING:** the capability may not enter a real-person or real-recipient pilot.
- **OPEN — SPECIALIST HOLDPOINT:** qualified review is required before the bounded claim or capability advances.
- **OPEN — INSTITUTIONAL GATE:** organizational authority, continuity, or governance evidence is required.
- **CURRENT BOUNDED FLOW:** a narrow current public or synthetic flow may continue with explicit limitations, monitoring, and revalidation.
- **CLOSED:** all named closure evidence and residual-risk disposition are recorded; no holdpoint is closed in this Sprint 5 revision.

## Cross-cutting closure rules

A holdpoint is not closed because:

- a vendor offers a feature;
- a document names the control;
- a founding-steward tabletop reaches a coherent answer;
- CI passes;
- an implementation exists in a branch or synthetic environment;
- a current public flow has not experienced a known incident; or
- a general policy appears compatible with the intended design.

Closure requires the exact evidence named in the row, a bounded reviewer or decision authority, a recorded residual-risk disposition, affected documentation and public-claim updates, and a revalidation trigger.

All holdpoints remain open except that `HLD-S5-022` records the current bounded Founding Expedition flow rather than authorizing expansion.

Sprint 5 completion preserves these gates. It does not satisfy or remove them.
