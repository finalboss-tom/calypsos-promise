# Gameplay Foundation — FROZEN DIRECTION

## Form of play

Calypso’s Promise is a narrative exploration and decision game built around an illustrated island map, unlockable zones, character encounters, quest cards, short interactive activities, personal visualizations, and persistent restoration.

It is not initially:

- A 3D open world
- A combat game
- An endless-scroll social application
- A generic dashboard with fantasy decoration

## First player

The first player is **people**.

This does not mean one undifferentiated experience. It means the entry promise is universal, while the route adapts to the person’s goals, capabilities, context, accessibility needs, and available data.

A player may temporarily follow one or more paths:

- Curious Explorer
- Habit Builder
- Symptom Detective
- Condition Manager
- Data Sovereignty Advocate
- Citizen Scientist
- Opportunity Seeker
- Caregiver or Legacy Steward

These are routing contexts, not permanent identities or eligibility gates.

## Daily session

A typical session lasts approximately three to eight minutes.

1. **Arrival** — the player returns to the Hearth and receives a clear briefing.
2. **Wayfinder Route** — Aster offers two or three relevant quests.
3. **Capture or action** — the player may tap, type, speak, photograph, import, learn, move, reflect, or review.
4. **Confirmation** — extracted information appears as a draft before storage.
5. **Feedback** — the island visibly responds and the Chronicle improves.
6. **Departure** — the player sees what changed, what was learned, what permission was used, and what may come next.

There is always a clear stopping point.

## Daily route composition

The route should normally include:

- One observation or confirmation quest
- One constructive health, recovery, or learning quest
- One story, sovereignty, or progression quest

The player can replace, defer, or reject a route without punishment.

## The Wayfinder Orb

A persistent interaction control allows voice, text, image, and document input.

Example input:

> I slept terribly. I went to bed around one, woke up twice, and got up at seven.

Aster proposes a structured draft, clearly marking approximate values and asking only useful clarifying questions. The record is stored only after confirmation.

## Progress dimensions

- **Vitality** — constructive actions and health learning, never body size, symptom absence, or moral worth
- **Chronicle** — continuity, context, source quality, and useful completeness
- **Fellowship** — optional public-good, research, open-source, and community contribution
- **Renown** — overall narrative progress
- **Laurels** — non-cash cosmetic and restoration rewards

Real compensation is never disguised as game currency.

## Narrative ranks

1. Castaway
2. Visitor
3. Seeker
4. Wayfinder
5. Chronicler
6. Steward
7. Investigator
8. Pathfinder
9. Promisekeeper
10. Keeper of Ogygia

Numerical levels may exist under these ranks, but rank progression must correspond to meaningful chapter transitions.

## Quest anatomy

Every quest definition must contain:

- Stable ID and version
- Public title and in-world title
- Zone and guide
- Player value statement
- Eligibility rules
- Required action or evidence
- Estimated time and accessibility variants
- Data categories touched
- Permission or purpose requirement
- Safety classification
- Completion rule evaluated by domain logic
- Feedback and reward
- Narrative consequence
- Deferral and refusal behavior
- Analytics hypothesis
- Clinical or content review state, where applicable

## Deterministic and generative boundaries

Deterministic:

- Eligibility
- Permissions
- Completion
- Rewards
- Unlocks
- Safety gates
- Story revelation order
- Canon facts

AI-assisted:

- Input extraction drafts
- Clarifying questions
- Plain-language explanations
- Personalized route presentation
- Recall summaries with record references
- Dialogue phrasing within approved scene boundaries

## Failure and return

There are no broken streak punishments.

A missed day may produce a **Broken Lantern**: a gentle return path that helps the player resume, reduce the route, change reminders, or reconsider their goal.

The system rewards return and adaptation rather than perfection.

## Endgame

After authored narrative content is completed, play continues through:

- The Hundred-Year Watch
- Personal experiments
- Longitudinal reflection
- New zones and stories
- Seasonal public-good expeditions
- Community and open-source contributions
- Research participation after governance gates are satisfied
- Stewardship and mentorship

The endgame must remain valuable without requiring commercial sharing.
