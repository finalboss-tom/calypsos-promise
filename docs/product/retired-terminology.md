# Retired Terminology — FROZEN

These terms may appear in historical source material but must not appear as current product strategy, user-facing copy, contributor instructions, or architecture names without being explicitly quoted and identified as historical.

| Retired term or claim | Reason | Approved replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Trojan horse | Conflicts with informed choice and transparent purpose | welcoming narrative, progressive disclosure, trust-building journey |
| Indoctrination | Conflicts with autonomy and the Law of the Open Hand | Fourteen Lanterns onboarding, guided introduction |
| Health-data vacuum | Frames people as extraction targets | longitudinal health platform, Living Chronicle |
| AI doctor / robot doctor | Overstates role and creates unsafe authority | Aster, AI guide, Scribe, Librarian, Wayfinder, Interpreter |
| Root cause analysis as a product conclusion | Claims stronger evidence than available | possible association, question worth investigating |
| Guaranteed causal inference | Scientifically and ethically unsupported | bounded causal research objective, descriptive association |
| Anonymous data as an absolute promise | Health data may remain re-identifiable | de-identified or privacy-protected data with stated limits |
| Sell your health data | Oversimplifies consent, product type, and compensation | consider a separately consented compensated opportunity |
| Your data is worth $X | Buyer or recruitment cost is not record value or guaranteed payout | opportunity-specific disclosed compensation |
| Uncontrolled clinical trials | Incorrect and unsafe framing | governed observational research or approved study |
| One-year data export lockup | Contradicts sovereignty and portability | export available according to documented operational safeguards |
| Cheaters permanently banned forever | Disproportionate and incompatible with due process | integrity review, proportionate restriction, appeal where appropriate |
| Sacred offense | Moralizes product enforcement | integrity or policy violation |
| MLM-style referrals / down-line rewards | Creates regulatory and trust risk | transparent referral or ambassador program, only if separately approved |
| Universal Health Score | Compresses health into a misleading judgment | domain-specific trends, Chronicle quality, player-chosen progress |
| Good/bad food or clean eating | Can shame and oversimplify nutrition | observed pattern, nourishment context, player-chosen experiment |
| Failed streak | Punitive framing | interrupted rhythm, Broken Lantern return flow |
| Immortality promise as a product claim | Not supportable | mythic horizon; healthy longevity research ambition |
| Blockchain/NFT as proof of ownership | Technology does not itself create legal or practical sovereignty | export, consent, audit, deletion, encryption, and portability controls |

## Enforcement

The canon and content validators must scan active public content for retired terms. Historical archives may contain them only when metadata includes `historicalContext: true` and the rendered experience labels the material accordingly.
