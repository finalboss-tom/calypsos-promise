## Primary directive

The player promise remains:

Every route, mechanic, data flow, model, tool, measurement, protocol, provider, funding relationship, newsletter surface, deployment, and governance action remains subordinate to that purpose.

## Accepted Longitudinal Intelligence doctrine

The accepted doctrine establishes that:

- the Living Chronicle precedes every model, score, summary, experiment, forecast, or recommendation;
- personal value comes first;
- AI proposes while the player confirms and deterministic domains validate and store;
- provenance, measurement identity, temporal identity, outcome authority, refusal, correction, deletion, export, and provider independence are permanent requirements;
- historical validity, current personal relevance, and current applicability remain separate;
- uncertainty, ambiguity, mixed outcomes, and inconclusive results are valid product states;
- experimentation remains optional and consequence-scaled;
- confirmation is itself an intervention policy with burden, opportunity cost, and possible harm exposure; and
- no causal or health authority expands beyond the exact evidence, context, consequence, and time horizon that earned it.

The doctrine is architecture and product authority only. It does not establish empirical personal value, qualified measurement, personal causal inference, production private-data processing, clinical behavior, or a production model or provider.
