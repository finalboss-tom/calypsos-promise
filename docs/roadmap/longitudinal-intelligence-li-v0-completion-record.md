## Acceptance-criterion assessment

### Internal consistency

**Supported.** The doctrine, staged plan, evidence kernel, traceability, holdpoints, and alignment review preserve the Product Constitution, Vision, Architecture Foundation, Living Chronicle, House of Keys, Aster, security, funding, consumer-first, provider-independent, and operational-simplicity baselines.

### Explicit and non-overlapping claim levels and stages

**Supported.** Claim levels LI-C0 through LI-C6 and validation stages LI-V0 through LI-V8 have distinct authority ceilings. The evidence kernel also permits `NONE` for architecture and validation records that create no personal claim.

### Human-readable and machine-readable evidence

**Supported at LI-V0.** The repository includes linked human-readable doctrine, plan, completion, traceability, holdpoint, and alignment records plus a machine-readable schema, synthetic packet, and deterministic validation script.

### First-class measurement, time, preference, context, missingness, refusal, burden, and applicability

**Supported architecturally.** The doctrine and kernel represent each domain without claiming that any measurement or method is qualified.

### Descriptive personal value before causal intelligence

**Supported.** LI-V2 is explicitly descriptive and LI-C0 through LI-C2 only. Personal experiment and bounded decision authority remain later, separately gated stages.

### Later-stage inactivity

**Supported.** LI-V1 through LI-V8 remain inactive. No production or empirical authority is created.

### Phase 0 and future sprint inheritance

**Supported.** LI-V0 remains a Phase 0 architecture workstream. Sprint 10 is limited to the universal shell, while Sprints 11, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18, and later carry specific bounded inheritance.

### Repository coherence

**Candidate supported.** The current-status, roadmap, architecture, issue, and pre-Sprint 10 surfaces are reconciled in the candidate package. Historical records remain unchanged.
