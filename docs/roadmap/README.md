Calypso’s Promise uses coordinated evidence-gated roadmaps:

- the root [Public Institutional Roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) defines product, organizational, economic, governance, and founder-independence phases;
- [Sprints](sprints.md) defines the design-to-build sequence and non-numbered institutional workstreams; and
- the [Longitudinal Intelligence Staged Validation Plan](longitudinal-intelligence-validation-plan.md) defines LI-V0 through LI-V8 without treating those stages as institutional phases or automatic numbered sprints.

Advancement is gate-based rather than calendar-based. A completed workstream, passing CI run, merge, simulation, preview, deployment, production route, study, or model result proves only its bounded evidence. It does not activate private capability, causal authority, independent certification, institutional phase completion, or the next sprint or validation stage.

Key future inheritance points are:

- Sprint 10 — presentation and application boundaries only;
- Sprint 11 — private Chronicle, correction, export, deletion, and manual-fallback foundations;
- Sprint 13 — source-linked recall and descriptive uncertainty;
- Sprint 14 — connector provenance, mapping loss, conflict, and provider replacement;

## Sprint 10 gate

Sprint 10’s accepted goal remains:

> Establish the browser, iOS, and Android playable application.

Implementation may begin only after a dedicated pre-Sprint 10 alignment review resolves application ownership, one cross-platform content package, accessibility, offline and synchronization states, authentication after the prologue, release and rollback, and the rule that no gameplay authority depends on client-side trust.

Sprint 10 does not automatically authorize private Chronicles, production House of Keys, production Aster, Longitudinal Intelligence beyond LI-V0, real health or voice capture, providers, connectors, analytics, payments, research, or production health-data operation.
