Calypso’s Promise uses coordinated evidence-gated roadmaps:

- the root [Public Institutional Roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) defines product, organizational, economic, governance, and founder-independence phases;
- [Sprints](sprints.md) defines the design-to-build sequence and non-numbered institutional workstreams; and
- the [Longitudinal Intelligence Staged Validation Plan](longitudinal-intelligence-validation-plan.md) defines LI-V0 through LI-V8 without treating those stages as institutional phases or automatic numbered sprints.

Advancement is gate-based rather than calendar-based. A completed workstream, passing CI run, merge, schema, simulation, preview, deployment, production route, study, or model result proves only its bounded evidence. It does not activate private capability, causal authority, independent certification, institutional phase completion, or the next sprint or validation stage.

## Pre-Sprint 10 alignment candidate

The candidate review recommends:

> **AUTHORIZED WITH NAMED HOLDPOINTS**

Its bounded decisions are:

- create `apps/game` as the Expo and Expo Router browser, iOS, and Android application;
- preserve `apps/site` as the public institutional site and prologue owner;
- create `packages/game-content` as one earned versioned public/synthetic content package;
- do not create a generic shared UI package without a second real consumer;
- keep local and offline state public, synthetic, temporary, clearable, and non-authoritative;
- implement the authentication-after-prologue boundary without selecting or activating a production identity provider;
- preserve keyboard, screen-reader, touch, switch, scaling, reflow, reduced-motion, reduced-data, and low-bandwidth paths;
- keep deployment, indexing, store distribution, official release, private capability, and Sprint 11 as separate decisions;
- prohibit analytics, profiling, inferred-preference authority, and client-side gameplay authority; and
- keep LI-V1 through LI-V8 inactive.

The candidate defines Sprint 10.1 through Sprint 10.10 but does not begin implementation.

Key future inheritance points remain:

- Sprint 10 — public/synthetic application and presentation boundaries only;
- Sprint 11 — private Chronicle, permission, correction, export, deletion, and manual-fallback foundations;
- Sprint 13 — source-linked recall and descriptive uncertainty;
- Sprint 14 — connector provenance, mapping loss, conflict, and provider replacement;

- Sprint 17 — clinical, safety, accessibility, and specialist-governance controls;
- Sprint 18 — human evidence and release-gate work where separately authorized; and
- Sprint 19 and later — no automatic Longitudinal Intelligence activation.
