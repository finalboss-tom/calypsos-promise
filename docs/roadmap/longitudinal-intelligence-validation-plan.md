## Purpose

This plan defines how Calypso’s Promise may advance from an accepted Longitudinal Intelligence doctrine to progressively stronger evidence without silently activating private data, personal experimentation, causal health claims, clinical behavior, or production authority.

These are validation stages, not institutional phases and not automatic numbered sprints. Each stage requires exact-revision evidence, explicit status, named holdpoints, and a separate acceptance decision.

Advancement is gate-based rather than calendar-based.

## Governing rules

- The Living Chronicle remains authoritative; models and analyses remain derivative.
- Personal value precedes research, commercial, product-engagement, or institutional value.
- AI proposes; the player confirms; deterministic domain services validate and store.
- Private health data remain outside public contributor workflows.
- No stage advances merely because an earlier stage passed, a sprint began, a model improved, funding arrived, or a deployment exists.
- Every claim is limited to its evidence class, context, consequence, and time horizon.
- Refusal, correction, deletion, export, non-AI fallback, provider replacement, and non-punitive return remain available.
- Human comprehension and demonstrated personal decision value are required before stronger personal inference.
- A later stage may narrow, suspend, or reject earlier assumptions without rewriting historical evidence.

## Claim ladder

The governing doctrine defines:

- **LI-C0 — Chronicle fact**
- **LI-C1 — Descriptive pattern**
- **LI-C2 — Hypothesis**
- **LI-C3 — Provisional personal choice**
- **LI-C4 — Qualified personal experiment result**
- **LI-C5 — Current bounded decision support**
- **LI-C6 — Clinical or research authority**

A validation stage may qualify only its explicitly named claim levels. Aster presentation, model confidence, provider status, institutional relationship, funding, or publication cannot raise the claim level.

## Stage sequence

### LI-V0 — Doctrine and evidence-kernel consolidation

**State:** Active under issue #73  
**Data:** Public and synthetic only  
**Authority ceiling:** Architecture and documentation direction only

Required work:

- integrate the accepted doctrine;
- establish this staged plan and LI-C0 through LI-C6;
- define canonical terms and result states;
- trace protected invariants into architecture requirements;
- name human-readable and machine-readable evidence requirements;
- map Phase 0 and future sprint inheritance;
- preserve private-data, clinical, research, provider, accessibility, and institutional holdpoints; and
- reconcile architecture, roadmap, and current-status entry points.

Exit requires internal consistency with the Product Constitution, Vision, Architecture Foundation, Living Chronicle, House of Keys, Aster, security, provider-independence, funding, and governance baselines. Observation, measurement, inference, preference, experimentation, and action must remain separate. Every later stage must remain inactive.

LI-V0 does not authorize measurement, private data, experimentation, causal claims, clinical behavior, Phase 0 exit, or Sprint 10.

### LI-V1 — Exact measurement qualification

**State:** Inactive  
**Data:** Public and synthetic only  
**Authority ceiling:** Qualified measurement; no causal claim

Required work:

- define one exact construct, instrument, version, item set, and recall period;
- define temporal unit, observation window, language, and assistance rules;
- implement deterministic scoring with calibration and reference-population identity;
- preserve measurement uncertainty, floor, ceiling, and response-shift limits;
- establish rights, licensing, continuity, replacement, export, and historical replay;
- publish synthetic fixtures; and
- establish an independent measurement-science review strategy.

Required attacks include scorer revision, missing items, floor and ceiling, daily versus weekly mismatch, waking episode versus calendar day, language or assistance change, provider discontinuity, historical recalculation, calibration change, and response shift.

Exit requires deterministic, versioned, replayable, explainable, exportable scores and explicit comparison failures. No provider may become canonical and no causal method may be represented as qualified.

### LI-V2 — Descriptive Longitudinal Intelligence

**State:** Inactive  
**Initial data:** Public synthetic longitudinal fixtures  
**Authority ceiling:** LI-C0 through LI-C2

Permitted capabilities include source-linked timelines, provenance inspection, correction and conflict views, missing-period detection, reproducible descriptive trends, routine and context summaries, data-quality explanations, source-linked recall, contradictions, hypothesis generation, question preparation, and exportable evidence packets.

Prohibited capabilities include diagnosis, treatment recommendation, causal language, “works for you” claims, hidden preference inference, automatic experiment activation, and provider or model authority.

Exit requires reproducibility from canonical records, visible correction history, visible conflicts and missingness, complete non-AI paths, accessible and low-bandwidth behavior, and demonstrated immediate and delayed understanding of description versus causality.

Any later use of private data remains blocked behind the applicable institutional Phase 0 exit and explicit privacy, security, legal, identity, storage, deletion, incident, provider, and user-control gates.

### LI-V3 — Synthetic protocol-family qualification

**State:** Inactive  
**Data:** Public synthetic only  
**Authority ceiling:** Synthetic protocol qualification only

One narrowly specified protocol family must be attacked under stable null, benefit, and harm; trend; seasonality; carryover; treatment order; measurement floor and ceiling; expectancy; response shift; fatigue-dependent missingness; treatment-dependent refusal; partial periods; preference revision; context drift; hidden effect modification; gradual decay; abrupt reversal; multiple change points; cycles; historical-prior conflict; outcome multiplicity; benefit–harm tradeoffs; short horizons; control opportunity cost; treatment exposure; and low information.

Required metrics include false benefit, false harm, missed harm, harm reachability, stable-benefit yield, inconclusive rate, decision delay, measurement burden, refusal, partial periods, benefit delivered and withheld, harm exposure, temporal stability, current-applicability sensitivity, value relative to nonexperimental choices, and provider-independent replay.

Exit requires prespecified error and harm limits, reachable harm under every allowed prior and stopping rule, prospective outcome selection, visible missingness and refusal, bounded future-use authority, current evidence able to overcome historical evidence, and plausible net personal value relative to acting provisionally, not acting, and deferring.

Synthetic qualification is never human, clinical, or production evidence.

### LI-V4 — Human formative comprehension and accessibility

**State:** Inactive  
**Data:** Public or explicitly synthetic scenarios  
**Authority ceiling:** Human-factors evidence only

Participants must be able to distinguish Chronicle facts, calculations, associations, hypotheses, causal results, draft and confirmed records, historical validity, current relevance, current applicability, “benefit so far,” “future effect unknown,” mixed results, inconclusive results, approximate ties, preference ambiguity, provisional action, nonuse, deferral, and experimentation.

Required evidence includes immediate and delayed comprehension, keyboard and screen-reader operation, reduced-motion and reduced-data behavior, language and literacy variation, lower-numeracy routes, assisted use, error recovery, refusal without penalty, framing and order sensitivity, burden, trust, and the exclusion effects of any robustness gate.

Exit requires usable refusal, correction, deletion, export, and direct nonexperimental choice; accurate understanding of authority boundaries; no systematic steering; and no classification of accessibility failure as preference uncertainty.

### LI-V5 — Low-consequence feasibility study

**State:** Inactive  
**Data:** Separately authorized low-consequence study  
**Authority ceiling:** Feasibility evidence only

Preconditions include the applicable Phase 0 exit authorization, privacy and security review, legal and research-classification determination, identity and private Chronicle controls, qualified measurement, independent protocol review, incident and stopping ownership, and an accepted low-consequence intervention boundary.

The study asks whether people understand the choices, can refuse and stop, can sustain capture, can correct records, understand missingness and partial periods, find results useful even when inconclusive, and receive enough personal decision value to justify burden.

It may not claim general efficacy, clinical recommendation, population benefit, stable personal effect, protocol-family validation beyond the study, or generalized production readiness.

### LI-V6 — Bounded personal-inference pilot

**State:** Inactive  
**Data:** Separately authorized private pilot  
**Authority ceiling:** LI-C4 for one exact accepted protocol only

The design requires one qualified player-confirmed primary outcome, prespecified supportive outcomes and harm sentinels, a frozen estimand and horizon, explicit context and co-interventions, a current-only reference analysis, prespecified stopping and maximum burden, refusal and partial-period states, carryover and order rules, no hidden historical borrowing, a player-confirmed value envelope, comparison with nonexperimental choices, provider-independent replay, and independent statistical review.

Required result states include benefit, harm, mixed, inconclusive, measurement-invalid, observation-invalid, preference changed, current applicability unknown, historically informative only, and person choice without causal conclusion.

Exit requires prespecified false-claim and missed-harm performance, reachable harm, no false reassurance from refusal, useful decision yield that justifies burden, immediate and delayed comprehension, context- and horizon-bounded results, and no pressure or clinical implication.

### LI-V7 — Independent specialist qualification

**State:** Inactive  
**Evidence:** Controlled evidence and protected review  
**Authority ceiling:** Controlled qualification for named protocol families only

Required review domains include measurement science, statistics and causal inference, privacy, security, accessibility, clinical boundaries, legal and regulatory matters, research ethics and classification, interoperability, operations and incident response, open-source continuity, provider concentration and exit, governance, and conflicts of interest.

Exit requires completed corrections, explicit remaining holdpoints, safe public findings where possible, protected details outside the public repository, provider exit and degraded operation, and no specialist approval represented beyond its actual scope.

### LI-V8 — Limited Longitudinal Intelligence release

**State:** Inactive  
**Data:** Production under separately accepted controls  
**Authority ceiling:** Only named LI-C4 or LI-C5 capabilities

Required product behavior includes visible claim-level labels; source and evidence access; assumptions and uncertainty; current relevance, applicability, horizon, and expiry; direct refusal, correction, deletion, and export; provider-independent replay; non-AI fallback; no hidden experiment default; no hidden preference, prior, or utility model; no clinical escalation; incident, monitoring, rollback, suspension, and retirement controls; and continuing post-release evaluation.

Permitted language:

> Under this exact protocol and recent context, the evidence supports a bounded conclusion about this outcome.

Prohibited language:

> Calypso knows what works for you.

Revalidation is required after measurement, scorer, provider, adapter, intervention, dose, outcome, preference, context, co-intervention, missingness, refusal, safety, accessibility, model, research, regulatory, legal, or implementation changes that can affect the qualified claim.

## Cross-stage stop and hold rules

Validation must stop, hold, narrow, or return to an earlier stage when:

- a protected invariant is violated;
- private data are processed without accepted authority;
- a result cannot be replayed;
- AI acquires canonical or action authority;
- refusal reduces core access;
- correction, deletion, or export cannot be honored;
- a provider becomes irreplaceable;
- measurement identity is unknown;
- a primary outcome is selected after results;
- treatment-dependent missingness is hidden;
- harm becomes unreachable;
- a favorable result depends on moving its target horizon;
- a historical prior dominates contradictory current evidence;
- an experiment is personally dominated by nonexperimental choices;
- preference instability becomes a product default;
- accessibility failure creates systematic exclusion;
- a clinical claim exceeds the evidence; or
- product, provider, sponsor, research, or engagement incentives reward favorable results over truthful results.

## Required evidence package

Every accepted stage must record:

- exact repository, schema, protocol, and analysis revisions;
- data and evidence classes;
- instrument and scorer identity;
- source and target context;
- primary outcome and selection time;
- analysis, stopping, missingness, refusal, carryover, and order rules;
- personal-value and burden assessment;
- result-state vocabulary;
- false-claim, missed-harm, and decision-yield evidence where applicable;
- accessibility, privacy, security, provider, and fallback boundaries;
- independent reviews and open holdpoints;
- permitted claim level;
- expiry and revalidation triggers;
- rollback or retirement procedure; and
- plain-language and machine-readable explanations.

Passing one evidence class does not imply another.

## Phase 0 and sprint inheritance

LI-V0 is a non-numbered Phase 0 institutional workstream. It informs the mandatory Phase 0 exit architecture audit but does not close Phase 0 or authorize Phase 1.

Before private-product Longitudinal Intelligence work begins, the Phase 0 exit record must explicitly classify which LI stages, components, and holdpoints enter the bounded Phase 1 architecture.

Sprint inheritance is bounded as follows:

- **Sprint 10** may establish cross-platform presentation and application boundaries; it does not implement LI-V1 through LI-V8.
- **Sprint 11** must preserve Chronicle, permission, correction, export, deletion, manual fallback, and authority foundations; it does not automatically authorize inference.
- **Sprint 13** is a major LI-V2 inheritance point for source-linked recall, provenance, contradiction, correction, and visible uncertainty.
- **Sprint 14** must preserve connector source, mapping, semantic-loss, conflict, correction, and provider-replacement evidence.
- **Sprint 15** is the principal planned LI-V2 descriptive-intelligence implementation point. Its safe experiment framework remains a proposal unless LI-V1, LI-V3, LI-V4, and all applicable institutional gates are satisfied.
- **Sprint 17** provides major LI-V4 and LI-V7 clinical, safety, accessibility, and specialist-governance inheritance.
- **Sprint 18** may carry LI-V4 and LI-V5 human evidence only under separately approved thresholds and recruitment authority.
- **Sprint 19 and later** do not automatically activate Longitudinal Intelligence. LI-V6 through LI-V8 remain separately gated.
