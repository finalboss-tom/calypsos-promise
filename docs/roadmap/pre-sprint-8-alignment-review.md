## Mission-to-website traceability

### Personal utility and control

The homepage and Promise explanation lead with:

> Build your Living Chronicle. Improve your health. Keep the key.

Personal utility and control come before institutional benefit.

### Public software and private personal data

The Trust Center, Open Forge, How It Works, and status views must distinguish public code and public/synthetic evidence from private future personal data.

### Personal value before secondary use

The visitor journey explains the personal loop before research, funding, partner, or institutional possibilities.

### Meaningful refusal and direct access

Narrative and direct modes remain optional. Essential content requires no story traversal, animation, audio, imagery, or mythology knowledge.

### Deterministic authority around AI

Aster and AI explanations distinguish proposal, player confirmation, deterministic validation, fallback, provenance, uncertainty, and current non-production status.

### MCP remains bounded tooling

Open Forge explains local public/synthetic tools, sources, limits, receipts, errors, compatibility, non-authority, and open holdpoints without presenting MCP as the product database or general agent authority.

### Provider and standards replaceability

The consumer-first explanation distinguishes standards support, planned connectors, active relationships, and production exchange. Providers and standards remain additive, attributed, versioned, and replaceable.

### Funding cannot purchase authority

Support views derive from canonical economics records and keep recognition separate from endorsement, recommendation, ranking, safety, guidance, permission, and governance.

### Complexity must earn its place

Sprint 8 remains one understandable site application with clear routes, content ownership, local build, and no premature content or service platform.

### Status describes evidence

Reusable status components distinguish live, experimental, planned, long-horizon, deferred, frozen, baseline, and proposed states where relevant.

## Binding implementation clarifications

### One public site

- `apps/site` remains the public website owner.
- Track 0A is migrated in place.
- Public URLs are preserved or deliberately redirected/retired.
- Empty applications, shared packages, CMSs, databases, and services are prohibited without later evidence.

### Equal narrative and direct paths

- Narrative mode may welcome visitors through Ogygia atmosphere and progressive disclosure.
- Direct mode exposes all essential information through conventional navigation and plain language.
- No visitor must animate, play, scroll through a cinematic sequence, hear audio, or understand mythology to access the Promise, trust, privacy, status, support, or contribution information.

### Repository-owned content authority

- Frozen and accepted repository records remain the source of truth.
- Website copy may summarize and explain; it may not silently create policy.
- Material claims link to controlling records or validated public data.
- Generated navigation and status views preserve source, status, uncertainty, and update ownership.

### Reusable capability status

- Live, experimental, planned, and long-horizon distinctions are visually obvious.
- A merged contract or completed sprint is not labeled as a live product capability without deployment evidence.
- Forge is described as a local contributor tool, not a consumer health feature.

### Trust Center is not certification

The initial Trust Center may organize rights, privacy, security disclosure, authority boundaries, provider and connector status, funding doctrine, open holdpoints, corrections, and challenge routes.

It must not imply independent certification, production security, legal compliance, clinical approval, operational reliability, or private-data processing.

### Open Forge inherits Sprint 7 truth

Open Forge explains the ten bounded tools, public/synthetic sources, local `stdio`, provenance, deterministic validation and generation, execution limits, receipts, errors, compatibility, prohibited capabilities, holdpoints, and ordinary non-MCP contribution path.

It does not expose protected security details or claim that local adversarial tests prove production safety.

### Funding transparency derives from canonical records

- Support and relationship views derive from canonical public economics records or honest empty states.
- Recognition remains separate from recommendation, provider preference, connector ranking, clinical endorsement, guidance, permission, and governance.
- A website sponsor model cannot become a second source of funding truth.

### Transaction surfaces remain disabled

No donation, sponsorship, checkout, payment, charitable, tax-deductible, nonprofit, public-benefit, refund, or financial-control surface activates until the legitimate recipient, custody, accounting, tax, privacy, refund, and payment-rail gates are accepted.

### Consumer-first and provider-respectful explanation

The site acknowledges substantial and evolving healthcare standards, avoids disparaging institutions or standards work, distinguishes source systems from Chronicle truth, preserves provenance and uncertainty, and avoids representing provider or connector relationships as live.

### Accessibility and performance are release criteria

Sprint 8 defines measurable baselines for semantic structure, keyboard operation, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, contrast, focus, responsive layout, image failure, low bandwidth, direct-navigation parity, automated checks, representative manual review, and asset budgets.

Passing automated checks alone is not affected-user validation or accessibility certification.

### Signup remains separately gated

The existing signup adapter is isolated from content and health-data flows. Sprint 8 either preserves it under explicit provider, retention, unsubscribe, correction, deletion, proxy-trust, abuse-control, incident, and privacy rules or retires it deliberately.

It cannot become account creation, health-data intake, research enrollment, donation processing, or an undeclared marketing database.

### No production expansion through website code

Sprint 8 does not activate accounts, real health-data capture, private Chronicles, production Aster, private MCP, provider calls, connectors, clinical workflows, research enrollment, governance voting, transactions, estate directives, or personal-data analytics.
