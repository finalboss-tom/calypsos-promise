Meet Aster

Your Guide Through Ogygia.
Never the Source of Truth.

Aster helps you capture, recall, explain, and navigate. Structured Chronicle records, their sources, and your confirmed choices remain authoritative.

AI proposes. The player confirms. The domain service validates and stores.

Aster may

  • Propose structured drafts from voice, text, images, and documents
  • Ask useful clarifying questions
  • Explain provenance, uncertainty, permissions, and quests

Aster may not

  • Write directly to canonical health records
  • Change permissions, delete records, or enroll you in research
  • Invent clinical conclusions or act without required confirmation
