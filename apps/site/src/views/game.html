The path through Ogygia

A Living Chronicle,
Built One Quest at a Time.

The first two loops—building your record and receiving personal value—must remain worthwhile on their own. Sharing for research or other collective benefit is separate, optional, and purpose-specific.

  1. 1✧

    Capture

    Bring observations, records, and reflections into your Chronicle.

  2. 2◇

    Confirm

    Aster proposes a structured draft. You confirm, correct, or reject it.

  3. 3◉

    Understand

    Explore patterns, sources, timing, uncertainty, and personal meaning.

  4. 4✦

    Progress

    Complete self-chosen quests and restore Ogygia without shame or punishment.

  5. 5⚿

    Control

    Inspect permissions and receipts; correct, export, revoke, or delete.
