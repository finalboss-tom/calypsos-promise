Signup privacy notice

Purpose: your email is requested only to send public Calypso’s Promise project updates and clear opportunities to inspect, play, review, or contribute.

What is collected

Email address, affirmative consent, the current policy version, signup source, and delivery timestamp. Do not submit personal health, account, security, legal, or other protected information.

Where it goes

Submissions are forwarded to a separately configured private mailing provider. They are not written to this public repository, GitHub issues, campaign ledgers, analytics, or application logs. When no private provider is configured, the site stores nothing and the signup fails closed.

Retention and choices

The selected provider must support unsubscribe, correction, and deletion. Every project email must include an unsubscribe route. Provider selection and final retention periods remain a deployment gate.
