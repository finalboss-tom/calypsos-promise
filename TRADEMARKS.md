# Identity, Provenance, and Truthful Claims

**Status:** The former broad trademark-reservation proposal is WITHDRAWN. This file now records the replacement direction established by Decision 0004.

Calypso’s Promise is intended to be a public-domain project. The name, world, lore, designs, software, schemas, and public institutional materials are not to be enclosed through a broad brand policy that makes ordinary forks or adaptations depend on permission from the canonical project.

Public-domain freedom does not permit false statements of fact.

## Canonical identity

The project may truthfully identify:

- the canonical repository selected by current governance
- canonical releases produced through the documented release process
- canonical project domains and accounts
- the operator of a specific hosted deployment
- reviews, audits, approvals, or capability gates that actually occurred

“Canonical” describes provenance and current governance. It does not mean that copying, adapting, hosting, translating, or commercially using the work requires permission.

## Forks and adaptations

Forks and adaptations may:

- use, modify, and redistribute public-domain project materials
- change the name, world, code, economics, governance, or product behavior
- provide commercial or noncommercial services
- describe their historical relationship to the canonical project accurately

They should identify their operator and version clearly so people can understand who is responsible for the service they are using.

## Claims that must remain truthful

A project, fork, service, study, token, marketplace, organization, or deployment must not falsely claim that it:

- is operated by the canonical project when it is not
- passed a canonical security, privacy, accessibility, clinical, research, governance, or release review when it did not
- uses a canonical version when it has materially diverged
- is endorsed by named people or institutions without evidence
- has authority to access personal information without a valid permission or legal basis
- is affiliated with a modern film adaptation, studio, director, actor, distributor, or rights holder when it is not

These are provenance and truth claims, not restrictions on lawful reuse of the underlying public-domain work.

## Verified releases and deployments

The canonical project should make authenticity independently verifiable through:

- signed tags and releases
- checksums and release manifests
- public maintainer and operator records
- documented build provenance
- published review evidence
- current capability status
- incident, recall, and correction records
- reproducible builds where practical

A verified deployment may display a time-bounded provenance or review statement only while it continues to meet the stated conditions.

## Safety or certification mechanisms

Qualified review may determine that a narrow certification, collective, or safety mark is useful for communicating verified compliance in health-related contexts.

Any future mechanism must be established through a new decision record and must:

- protect a specific factual certification rather than ownership of the public-domain world
- permit forks, adaptations, scholarship, criticism, parody, and commercial reuse
- publish qualification, renewal, suspension, appeal, and removal rules
- be controlled by accountable governance rather than permanent founder discretion
- include anti-capture and sunset review
- avoid becoming a paywall around the commons

No such mechanism is currently selected or approved.

## Modern adaptations

Ancient source material may be public domain while a modern adaptation’s screenplay, performances, visual design, music, logos, footage, stills, and promotional materials remain protected.

Calypso’s Promise must use independent original expression or verified public-domain sources and must not imply affiliation with a modern adaptation.

## Personal information

No identity or provenance policy grants access to personal health data, user content, credentials, or account records.

The software and world may be public. The person remains protected by the Product Constitution, the Seven Laws, actual permissions, and applicable law.

## Historical note

The previous version of this file proposed reserving the Calypso’s Promise name, primary logos, official Ogygia identity, Aster’s official identity, and official-deployment claims.

That proposal was reasonable as an attempt to reduce impersonation and unsafe forks, but it conflicted with the clarified public-domain direction. It is preserved in repository history and was withdrawn by `docs/decisions/0004-public-domain-commons.md`.
