# Trademark and Official Deployment Policy

**Status: PROPOSED**

The source code may be used under its applicable software license. That permission does not grant a right to imply that a fork, deployment, service, study, token, data marketplace, or organization is endorsed by or is the official Calypso’s Promise project.

## Reserved identity

The following are reserved pending formal registration and legal review:

- Calypso’s Promise name and primary logos
- Official Ogygia identity and project seals
- Aster’s official identity and official character depictions
- “Official Calypso’s Promise” and equivalent representations

## Permitted descriptive use

Accurate statements such as “a fork of the Calypso’s Promise open-source software” are permitted when they:

- Clearly identify the fork or operator
- Do not imply endorsement
- Link to the upstream project
- Preserve required license notices
- Do not copy official branding in a confusing manner

## Official deployment requirements

Only deployments designated by project governance may use official status. An official deployment must:

- Follow the Product Constitution and Seven Laws
- Publish its operator and material subprocessors
- Keep production health data out of the public repository
- Support export, deletion, and inspectable permissions
- Disclose AI, research, and commercial capabilities honestly
- Pass the project’s current security and safety release gates

## Fork naming

Forks should choose a distinct name, logo, domain, and application-store identity. They may retain factual attribution in documentation.

## No restriction on lawful criticism

This policy is not intended to prevent commentary, criticism, parody, scholarship, or other lawful nominative uses.

A final enforceable trademark policy requires review by qualified counsel.
