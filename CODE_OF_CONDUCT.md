# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, accessible, and harassment-free community for every person, regardless of background, identity, health status, disability, experience, or technical skill.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with dignity and patience.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never request or expose personal health information.
- Use accessible, plain language where practical.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Correct errors openly and without retaliation.
- Respect consent, boundaries, and a person’s right to disengage.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing, disclosure of private information, health shaming, coercive recruitment, deceptive claims, and retaliation are not tolerated.

## Health and research discussions

Community discussions must not diagnose individuals, pressure people to disclose health information, or present speculation as medical fact. Research and health claims should state uncertainty and cite appropriate evidence.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Security vulnerabilities must follow `SECURITY.md` and should not be reported in public issues.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.
